How this works & gets more accurate

  • The Strait uses our custom entrance model (calibrated on 3 confirmed sessions + 2.5 yrs of buoy data). Surfline has no cam or forecaster at the strait, so their rating there is a basic height+wind fallback that can't see the swell-angle keyhole — shown only as a footnote.
  • Westport & Short Sands lean on Surfline's rating as the primary signal — those are cam-covered, forecaster-corrected spots where LOTUS embeds decades of observations. Our value-add is the clean-mix score (0–10): swell period + groundswell-vs-windwave energy fraction + local wind. The working hypothesis (Matt's): unusually good days are the ones Surfline calls Fair-or-better while the mix is clean — those get the green "standout" tier.
  • Every morning run logs model score, clean score, and groundswell fraction per spot-day. As dates pass we compare against buoys (46267, 46211) and your session reports to test the standout hypothesis and tune thresholds — changes go through Matt, never silently.
  • Surfline "Good"/"Epic" ratings are human-forecaster-assigned and rare — if one appears at any spot, that alone is alert-worthy.
