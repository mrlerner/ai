Does money still matter 20 days out?

Yes, but the window closes around October 25, and what the money buys has changed.

  • TV is mostly booked. The big reservations went in weeks ago. Candidate committees still get the federally required lowest unit rate in the final 60 days, so a dollar to a campaign buys more airtime than a dollar to a super PAC. Give to the candidate directly, not to a PAC or a bundled slate.
  • Late money funds digital and turnout. Digital ads place same-day. Paid canvassers, texting, and mail to low-propensity voters are what move a one-point race. That favors Kansas and Iowa, where margins are thin.
  • Early voting is already open in most of your target states. Once a large share of ballots are in, persuasion money is wasted and only turnout money counts.
  • Federal limit is $3,500 per candidate for the general election. State limits for governor vary.
