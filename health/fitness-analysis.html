VO2max — the remaining gap

Apple Watch puts you at 38 ml/kg/min, which is "good" for a 50yo male but below the longevity target. The mortality curves (Mandsager 2018 JAMA, 122k patients) are steep here: moving from 50th → 80th percentile VO2max is associated with roughly a 2× all-cause mortality reduction. Zone 2 volume alone won't move VO2max much — you need brief Zone 5 work.

VO2max percentile bands for age-50 male. Current 38 → target 46+.

Does not interfere meaningfully with strength if placed 24h+ away from lower-body lifting days.

Sleep — deep sleep is the recovery lever

Sleep duration and efficiency are fine. The specific thing below target is deep (slow-wave) sleep — averaging 48 min/night against a 60–90 min target. Only 24% of nights hit the 1-hour mark. Deep sleep is when growth hormone and testosterone pulses happen, and when the glymphatic system clears metabolic waste from the brain. At 50, this matters.

Supplement changes

  • Add: Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia), 200mg/day standardized extract, 12 weeks.
    • Form: insist on a standardized extract — "Physta" or "LJ100" on the label, or "100:1 water extract." Ground root powder needs ~1–2g for equivalent effect, and quality varies wildly. Reputable brands: Double Wood, Nootropics Depot, Nutricost.
    • Timing: morning with food. Mildly stimulating — not a bedtime supplement.
    • Evidence base: Talbott 2013 and Henkel 2014 both used 200mg/day standardized extract; reported modest T increases and stress-hormone reductions.
    • Escalation: if no subjective or lab change at 8 weeks, bump to 400mg/day. Don't start high.
    • Cycling: optional. 12 weeks continuous is the standard trial length.
  • Keep: boron 3mg/day (same mechanism, additive).
  • Keep: magnesium glycinate (modest SHBG-lowering, helps deep sleep).
  • Skip: ashwagandha (better for stressed populations — not you), fenugreek (mixed evidence), zinc (multivitamin covers it).

90-day sequence

Sequenced intentionally — don't try to change everything at once.

Weeks 1–4 (now — early cut)

  • Add 1× Peloton 4×4 per week. Place 24h+ from lower-body lift. Start at 85% effort for first 2 sessions, then push to 90–95%.
  • Sleep environment fix: thermostat 64°F, last meal ≥3h pre-bed, caffeine cutoff 10am.
  • Keep lifting 3–4×/week, yoga 2×. Cut on 300–500 kcal deficit, 1g protein per lb.

Weeks 5–8 (mid-cut)

  • HIIT is now habitual — can push to 95% on intervals 3 & 4. Measure Apple Watch VO2max; expect +1–2 points by now.
  • Sleep habits should be paying dividends — deep sleep averaging ≥60 min.
  • Keep HMB during cut (proven muscle-sparing at deficit).

Weeks 9–12 (cut floor / rebulk transition)

  • Labs at cut floor (when you hit your low weight): Total T, Free T, SHBG, E2, LH/FSH, A1c, ApoB, hs-CRP. Captures the "worst case" snapshot.
  • Don't camp at the floor — begin active rebulk within 2–4 weeks of hitting it.
  • Target VO2max 41–43 by Apple Watch.
  • Tongkat Ali 12-week trial ends — decide to continue based on lab response.

Weeks 13–20 (rebulk + second lab draw)

  • 200–300 kcal surplus, carbs back to 40%+, fats 25–30%.
  • Labs at week 8 of rebulk: same panel. Compare Free T and SHBG to both baseline (42/50) and cut-floor values. Full recovery = cut was metabolically fine. Persistent suppression = revisit plan.
  • Consider Stelo for curiosity only (how does overnight oats & protein shake behave?).

What I'm not recommending, and why

  • TRT / hormone replacement: no symptoms, T in range, LH/FSH normal (axis intact). Intervening here would be premature — try lifestyle + supplements first, retest in 12 weeks.
  • Rapamycin / metformin: no metabolic rationale given actual A1c/glucose. Risk/evidence ratio too thin.
  • Berberine / GLP-1: same — nothing to fix.
  • More strength days: 3–4× is the sweet spot for recovery at 50. Adding #5 would push HRV the wrong direction.
  • More cardio beyond 1× HIIT: would introduce interference with strength during bulk. Surf provides plenty of Zone 1–2 already.
