NY Philharmonic: Gustavo Dudamel's Inaugural at Radio City
TOMORROW — THU SEP 10, 7:30PM  ·  RADIO CITY MUSIC HALL, MIDTOWN  ·  FROM ~$75
Gustavo Dudamel's first concert as the New York Philharmonic's Music Director — the most anticipated classical music event New York has had in years. The program pairs Philip Glass, Gershwin, Bernstein, and Jessie Montgomery with guest performers Lizzo, St. Vincent, Aaron Tveit, and Joaquina; Liev Schreiber hosts. After the concert, Dudamel and the orchestra continue the party at The Rainbow Room, 65 floors up at 30 Rock, with Mark Ronson DJing. A "be there" night that will be talked about for years.
How to get in: Tickets at nyphil.org or secondary market (StubHub). The Rainbow Room after-party is a separate gala ticket.

Sounds of Remembrance — 9/11 25th Anniversary Concert
FRI SEP 11, DOORS 11AM, CONCERT 3:30PM  ·  PERELMAN PERFORMING ARTS CENTER, LOWER MANHATTAN  ·  FREE
Dudamel leads the New York Philharmonic and the United States Army Field Band in a free commemorative concert marking 25 years since September 11. The program includes Beethoven, Tchaikovsky, Barber, and the world premiere of a new work commissioned specifically for the occasion, composed by Julia Adolphe with texts by Safiya Sinclair. The Perelman Performing Arts Center — a relatively new venue at 251 Fulton Street, directly across from the September 11 Memorial pools — is a stunning room for this. Seating is limited and first come, first served; members of the September 11 community have priority reservation access.
Get in: Free tickets (reserve in advance) at nyphil.org — limited capacity, arrive early.

Chelsea galleries traditionally open new shows Thursday evenings with free wine from 6–8pm — walk a route hitting four to eight spaces in an evening, no tickets, no reservations. Saturday afternoon is a quieter but equally valid time to go — the weather is forecast at 76°F and mostly sunny. Good pairing: Chelsea galleries, then dinner at Graciela, Capitaine, or Cleo in the West Village (10 minutes south).

Grouper at Town Hall
TUE SEP 15, 8PM  ·  THE TOWN HALL, 123 W 43RD ST (MIDTOWN)  ·  TICKETS AVAILABLE
Grouper — the stage name of Portland-based musician Liz Harris, known for hazy, emotionally resonant ambient folk recordings — performs at Town Hall, a 1,500-seat Midtown landmark with excellent acoustics.
