100th Feast of San Gennaro — Opens Thursday
OPENS THU SEP 17 THROUGH SUN SEP 27 · MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY · FREE · OUTDOOR — 80° & SUNNY THURSDAY
This year marks the 100th anniversary of the Feast of San Gennaro, New York's iconic Italian street festival — eleven blocks of Mulberry Street (between Canal and Houston) taken over by 300-plus vendors serving cannoli, zeppole, sausage and peppers. It runs 11am–11pm weekdays. The religious procession happens Saturday September 19 (the saint's actual feast day), with Mass at the National Shrine of San Gennaro followed by the statue carried through the streets with marching bands. Perfect weather on Thursday for the opening day.

Indigenous Enterprise at the Joyce Theater
SEP 16–21 · THE JOYCE, CHELSEA · $10–$59 · INDOOR
The Joyce Theater in Chelsea — New York's dedicated contemporary dance house — opens its fall season with Indigenous Enterprise, a company that weaves authentic powwow dance traditions (grass dance, fancy dance, jingle dress) into full-scale contemporary performance with striking regalia.
