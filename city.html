Krasner and Pollock: Past Continuous
THE MET, 1000 FIFTH AVE — UPPER EAST SIDE · $30 SUGGESTED · THRU JAN 31, 2027 · INDOOR / RAIN-PROOF
Today is the first public day at the Metropolitan Museum of Art's major fall blockbuster: a dual survey of Lee Krasner and Jackson Pollock, two giants of Abstract Expressionism who were also married to each other. The show reframes the pair by finally giving Krasner equal billing — long overdue — and includes Pollock's Autumn Rhythm: Number 30, one of his definitive works, alongside major loans from around the world. Member previews ran Oct 1–3; opening-day energy is real. Today's rain (arriving after 11am) makes this the ideal plan: head up to the Met early morning before the showers, or commit to an indoor afternoon. The Met is open Sunday. Walkable from the Upper West Side along Fifth Avenue. Runs through January — but first-day visits always feel different.

ALICE TULLY HALL + ELINOR BUNIN MUNROE FILM CENTER, LINCOLN CENTER · ~$35/TICKET · UPPER WEST SIDE
The 64th annual New York Film Festival at Lincoln Center closes Sunday Oct 12 — this is the last full week. The films worth catching now:

Standby lines form 30 minutes before screenings and often get in. Filmmaker Q&As are standard at most screenings.
