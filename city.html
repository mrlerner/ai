MoMA PS1: Greater New York 2026 — 10 days left
CLOSES AUG 17 · LONG ISLAND CITY, QUEENS · INDOOR / AIR CONDITIONED · ~$20
MoMA PS1's once-every-five-years survey of artists living and working in New York City — the sixth edition, coinciding with PS1's 50th anniversary. 53 artists, most shown in NYC for the first time. The Frieze review called it "scrappy, political, and paranoid," and it has generated more critical discussion than any PS1 show in years. Sunday (Aug 9) and Monday (Aug 10) are both clear and hot — the best remaining days before the deadline. The Noguchi Museum is a 10-minute walk for a full Queens art afternoon.
How to get in: $20 at the door; free with MoMA membership. Closed Tuesdays. G train to Court Square-23rd St or 7 train to 45th Rd-Courthouse Square.

Battery Dance Festival — opens Monday, through Aug 16, free
AUG 10–16 · ROBERT F. WAGNER JR. PARK, BATTERY PARK AREA · FREE · OUTDOOR / EVENINGS
New York City's longest-running free outdoor dance festival, now in its 45th year — and this edition also marks 50 years of Battery Dance as a company. International and multicultural lineup: dance traditions from across the globe, performed in the park facing New York Harbor at the southern tip of Manhattan. Shows at 7pm Tuesday through Saturday; community workshops Monday 4–7pm. Monday Aug 10 is clear and a great night to make the downtown trip.
Indoor fallback if an evening turns stormy: the Museum of Jewish Heritage is one block away and open late on Thursdays.
