45th Annual Battery Dance Festival
AUG 10–16 — OPENS TOMORROW  ·  ROBERT F. WAGNER JR. PARK, BATTERY PARK AREA  ·  FREE  ·  OUTDOOR
This is a milestone edition of a New York summer staple — 45 years of Battery Dance's annual free outdoor festival, this year celebrating the company's 50th anniversary and the tenure of founder Jonathan Hollander. More than 40 companies perform across the week, representing dance traditions from across the world: contemporary, classical Indian, hip hop, folk, and more. Monday (tomorrow) is free community workshops at 4, 5, 6, and 7pm. Performances run Tuesday–Saturday at 7pm at the park at the southern tip of Manhattan near the Staten Island Ferry. Best nights: Friday (80°F, mostly sunny) and Saturday (79°F, sunny). If Mon's heat and storms keep you indoors, Smoke Jazz Club on the Upper West Side is the fallback.

Cécile McLorin Salvant: Tin Pan Alley
AUG 12–16  ·  THE AMPH AT LITTLE ISLAND, PIER 55  ·  $25  ·  8:30PM  ·  OUTDOOR
Cécile McLorin Salvant is a three-time Grammy-winning jazz vocalist widely considered one of the finest interpreters of American song working today. This week she curates a program around Tin Pan Alley — the early-20th-century New York songwriting district that gave the world nearly every American standard still being sung. She and her pianist Sullivan Fortner headline Friday (Aug 15) and Saturday (Aug 16) — the nights to go. Wednesday and Thursday feature an all-star supporting lineup including jazz singers Lillias White and Billy Stritch and saxophonist George Coleman. The outdoor amphitheater sits on a floating park in the Hudson River. Friday and Saturday: 80°F and 79°F, mostly sunny — ideal. Tickets on TodayTix.
