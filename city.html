NYFF64 Opening Night: Paper Tiger
TOMORROW FRI SEP 25  ·  ALICE TULLY HALL, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR
The 64th New York Film Festival (Film at Lincoln Center's annual showcase, the city's biggest film event) opens tomorrow with the US premiere of James Gray's crime drama Paper Tiger in 35mm — Scarlett Johansson, Adam Driver, and Miles Teller are expected on the red carpet. Fall for Dance Festival — 23rd Edition
THROUGH OCT 3  ·  NEW YORK CITY CENTER (W. 55TH ST)  ·  INDOOR  ·  ALL TICKETS $30
New York City Center's annual festival that packs five mixed bills into ten days at $30 a ticket — the city's best per-dollar introduction to contemporary and classical dance. This year's lineup: Martha Graham Dance Company, A.I.M by Kyle Abraham, Boston Ballet, The Royal Ballet, and international companies from Spain, Israel, Germany, and Australia. KRAMER/FAUCI — Opens Sunday at St. Ann's Warehouse
OPENS SUN SEP 27 — THRU OCT 24  ·  ST. ANN'S WAREHOUSE, DUMBO, BROOKLYN  ·  INDOOR
Daniel Fish (the director who turned Oklahoma! into a psychological thriller) returns to St. Ann's Warehouse with a verbatim-transcript drama staging the 1993 televised confrontation between Dr. Anthony Fauci and playwright/AIDS activist Larry Kramer. Will Brill plays Fauci, Thomas Jay Ryan plays Kramer. Opens this Sunday — rain day, perfect timing to book ahead and head to DUMBO. St. Ann's Warehouse is one of the best rooms in the city for this kind of work.

Jim Henson's 90th Birthday Bash — Museum of the Moving Image
THROUGH SUN SEP 27  ·  MUSEUM OF THE MOVING IMAGE, ASTORIA, QUEENS  ·  INDOOR
The Museum of the Moving Image in Astoria (the country's leading museum dedicated to film and TV) is throwing a four-day 90th-birthday celebration for Jim Henson: screenings of The Muppet Show, puppet-making workshops, collector swap meets, panels, live music, and a costume contest. Runs through Sunday — rainy weekend, perfect museum day. The museum itself is an underrated institution that holds the permanent collection of moving-image artifacts; if you've never been, this is a good excuse.
