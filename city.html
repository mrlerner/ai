Return of the Buffalo: Tatanka Owe Akupi
FRI SEP 18 (DUMBO) + SAT SEP 19, 5–7:30PM (LOWER MANHATTAN) · FREE · OUTDOOR — FORECAST 76°, SLIGHT RAIN CHANCE SAT
50 life-size buffalo puppets will march through the streets of Lower Manhattan on Saturday, departing from Bowling Green at 5pm and ending at Foley Square at 7:30pm — with Rhiannon Giddens, Laurie Anderson, Pilobolus, Native Pride Dancers, and a Brazilian percussion ensemble performing along the route. It's a free, street-level spectacle created with Lenape and Lakota tribal elders; no tickets, no venue, just show up. St. Ann's Warehouse (the DUMBO theater known for ambitious international productions) presents it. Friday's route goes through Downtown Brooklyn. Rain chance Saturday is only about 13%, but if it pours the event may feel less magical — check the morning of. Indoor fallback: take a cab to the Joyce Theater in Chelsea for the final weekend of Ron K. Brown's dance company.

NY Philharmonic: Dudamel Conducts Prokofiev & John Adams
WED–SAT SEP 16–19 · DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER · FROM ~$40 · WALK FROM UWS
The second program of Gustavo Dudamel's inaugural season as the New York Philharmonic's music director: Prokofiev's Fifth Symphony (the great WWII-era triumph), new work by Zosha Di Castri, and John Adams. Geffen Hall is a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side and has been dramatically renovated — great sightlines from most seats. Four performances this week (Wed–Sat); mid-week options have more availability and same music.

100th Annual Feast of San Gennaro
OPENS THU SEP 17, RUNS THROUGH SEP 27 · MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY · FREE ENTRY
The centennial. The Feast of San Gennaro has run every September since 1926 — 11 days of Italian food stalls, zeppole, street games, and processions along Mulberry Street in Little Italy. This year's 100th anniversary brings extra programming and the Grand Procession on September 26. The feast day mass is September 19 at 2pm at the Most Precious Blood Church on Mulberry. It's ideal for a Thursday evening stroll — the first weekend always has the biggest crowds. Open 11:30am–11:30pm daily, midnight on Fri/Sat.

Taryn Simon — New Survey, Guggenheim Museum
OPENS FRI SEP 18, THRU MAR 2027 · GUGGENHEIM, UPPER EAST SIDE · ~$30 · PAIR WITH CAFÉ SABARSKY
A major new survey of Taryn Simon, the New York photographer and conceptual artist best known for projects exploring institutional power, hidden systems, and documentary form. The Guggenheim's rotunda is one of the best places in the city to see large-scale photography. Opens Friday, runs through March — no rush to go this week, but the first weekend is quiet before word spreads. Pair with lunch at Café Sabarsky, the Viennese café at the Neue Galerie one block south.
