US Open — Golden Weekend Window
FRI–MON, FLUSHING MEADOWS  ·  ROUND OF 16 & QUARTERS  ·  OUTDOOR ☀️
Friday through Labor Day is a four-day perfect-weather window (78–82°F, sunny, under 10% precip) that lines up exactly with the Round of 16 and quarterfinals at Arthur Ashe Stadium — the most competitive tennis of the tournament short of semis and finals. The proven play: buy secondary-market tickets (StubHub, SeatGeek, or Gametime's "last-minute deals" tab) 5–10 minutes after a session starts. Prices collapse as sellers dump inventory — center-court seats at a fraction of face. Night sessions at Ashe are the best experience; day sessions collapse hardest on weekdays if there are early exits. Amex cardmember lounge on-site.

Chelsea Gallery Season Opens
THIS WEEK  ·  CHELSEA & TRIBECA  ·  FREE  ·  INDOOR OR OUTDOOR WALK
September is the unofficial start of the New York art season — galleries in Chelsea (West 20s) launch fall shows with opening receptions, usually Thursday evenings and Fridays. No tickets, no reservation, free champagne. The walk from gallery to gallery along West 24th-26th streets is one of the more quietly satisfying ways to spend an afternoon or early evening in the city, and the quality of work on opening weekend is often the highest of the year. Pair with dinner in the West Village afterward.
