NY Philharmonic: Dudamel Conducts Prokofiev
TUE–FRI SEP 16–18, 7:30 PM  ·  DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  FROM $35
Gustavo Dudamel's first regular-season concerts as Music Director at Geffen Hall — the UWS home stage, a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side. The program pairs Prokofiev's Symphony No. 5 (among the great 20th-century symphonies) with John Adams's "On the Transmigration of Souls" — his 9/11 memorial piece, written for the Philharmonic, premiered on the exact date 25 years ago. Plus a world premiere by Canadian composer Zosha Di Castri. Three nights this week; Tuesday through Thursday are all identical programs. Perfect 69–78° weather midweek.

Titanique — Closing Sunday
CLOSES SUN SEP 20  ·  ST. JAMES THEATRE, MIDTOWN  ·  FROM $87 — OR 2-FOR-1 BROADWAY WEEK
Last week to see this one. Titanique is a campy jukebox parody of the Titanic film, told through the lens of Céline Dion — who shows up as a character and narrates the whole disaster through her greatest hits. Transferred from a cult off-Broadway run to the St. James Theatre in March and is closing out its Broadway run next Sunday. Part absurdist comedy, part deeply committed Celine tribute. Very fun.

The Joyce Theater: The Surge — An Ode to Sinéad O'Connor
SEP 16–27, EVENINGS  ·  THE JOYCE, 175 8TH AVE, CHELSEA  ·  OPENING THIS WEEK
The Joyce Theater — Chelsea's dedicated contemporary dance house — opens its fall season this week with a new work honoring Sinéad O'Connor, who died in 2023. A shorter cab ride from the Upper West Side than it sounds, and a good pairing with dinner at Graciela or Cleo (both in the West Village, a 10-minute walk). The Joyce is one of the few places in the city where you can reliably see world-class contemporary dance at reasonable prices.

Feast of San Gennaro — 100th Anniversary
SEP 17–27  ·  MULBERRY ST, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE ENTRY  ·  OUTDOOR — CHECK FORECAST LATE WEEK
The 100th running of the Feast of San Gennaro — one of the longest-running and largest Italian-American street festivals in the country, spilling down Mulberry Street between Canal and Houston for 11 days. The centennial edition promises expanded programming, 300-plus vendors, live music, and the Grand Procession on Saturday, September 19. Open 11am–11pm weekdays, until midnight weekends. Saturday has a 40% rain chance — check the forecast before heading south; the first Thursday (Sep 17) looks ideal at 78° and mostly sunny.
