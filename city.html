The Round of 16 at the United States Open Tennis Championships is the tournament's loudest weekend — every stadium session is full, the crowd is electric, and the stakes are real. Saturday and Sunday are sunny and 74–78°F, exactly what outdoor tennis was designed for. The move, as proven in 2025: buy on StubHub, SeatGeek, or Gametime 5–15 minutes after sessions start. West Indian American Day Carnival — Labor Day Weekend
SAT SEP 5 + MON SEP 7  ·  EASTERN PARKWAY, CROWN HEIGHTS, BROOKLYN  ·  FREE
One of the largest Caribbean cultural celebrations in North America, drawing millions to Eastern Parkway over Labor Day weekend. Saturday is Junior Carnival and Youth Fest near the Brooklyn Museum. Monday (Labor Day) is the main masquerade parade — elaborate costumes, soca and calypso, street food running the full length of the parkway from Utica Avenue to Grand Army Plaza. Perfect weather for it: 77°F and sunny all day. A high-energy, visually spectacular New York tradition that most Manhattanites never get to.
