Sara Bareilles: Good Grief — Stories and Song
FRI AUG 28 – SUN AUG 30  ·  FREE  ·  LINCOLN CENTER THEATER LOBBY (UPPER WEST SIDE)
Sara Bareilles — the singer-songwriter behind "Love Song" and the Broadway musical Waitress — releases a long-awaited new studio album this Friday and is marking it with a free three-day installation at the Vivian Beaumont lobby inside Lincoln Center Theater. She’ll perform live each day on a Steinway piano surrounded by hundreds of handwritten anonymous stories of grief from The Strangers Project, an ongoing real-world collection. Fans have also contributed audio recordings. This is not a PR stunt — it’s genuinely unusual and intimate for a pop album launch, and the Upper West Side location is hard to beat. Hours: Fri 3–8pm, Sat 10am–6pm, Sun 10am–3pm. Fully indoor, no tickets needed, first-come.
