Feast of San Gennaro — 100th Year
THROUGH SEP 27  ·  MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE ADMISSION  ·  OUTDOOR — BEST TODAY & SAT
The century mark for New York's biggest street festa — 11 days on Mulberry Street between Canal and Houston, with 300-plus vendors, the Grand Procession, and the Napoli Expo bringing three acclaimed artists from Naples. The saint's day procession happens tomorrow (Saturday Sep 19). Today and Saturday are ideal: sunny and 70–80°F before the rain settles in Sunday. Go for the cannoli and sausage-pepper heroes; the cannoli eating contest is a legitimate spectacle.
Rain fallback: If Sunday's storms catch you mid-festival, duck into Ferrara Cafe on Grand Street — the oldest Italian pastry shop in the US — or take the rest of the day downtown to a museum.

Met Opera Season Opener: Verdi's Macbeth
OPENS MON SEP 22  ·  METROPOLITAN OPERA, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  RUNS THROUGH OCT 20
The Metropolitan Opera's 2026–27 season opens Monday night with a new production of Verdi's Macbeth — baritone Quinn Kelsey in the title role, soprano Lise Davidsen as Lady Macbeth (she's the big name this year), conducted by Yannick Nézet-Séguin. Director Louisa Proske makes her Met debut. Monday's opening-night tickets will be expensive; subsequent performances through October run the same cast and are substantially easier to get. The rainy week ahead makes this ideal: nothing better than the Met on a Monday night in the rain.

NY Philharmonic: Dudamel Conducts Mahler's Fifth
SEP 24–29  ·  DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  TICKETS ON SALE NOW
Gustavo Dudamel — in his inaugural season as the New York Philharmonic's Music Director — conducts Mahler's Fifth Symphony alongside a world premiere by composer Tania León. Mahler Five is one of the great emotional experiences in orchestral music; hearing it under Dudamel, in Geffen Hall's renovated acoustic, is a genuine occasion. Midweek performances (Wed/Thu) are typically the best seats for the price. Rain-proof and a five-minute walk from the Upper West Side.
