Met Opera Season Opener: Verdi's Macbeth
MON SEP 22, OPENING NIGHT  ·  LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  UWS HOME TURF
The Metropolitan Opera's 2026-27 season kicks off Monday night with a new production of Verdi's Macbeth — the most anticipated opening in years. Soprano Lise Davidsen, one of the biggest voices in opera right now, takes the stage as Lady Macbeth, with baritone Quinn Kelsey in the title role. Music director Yannick Nézet-Séguin conducts, and director Louisa Proske makes her Met debut helming the production. The run goes through October 20; opening night is the moment. Lincoln Center is a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side.
Getting in: Opening night may have premium pricing; subsequent performances (next dates: Sep 24, 27, Oct 1) are easier to book and still feature the same cast. Check metopera.org for availability; the Live in HD cinema broadcast is Oct 17 if you'd rather see it big-screen.

NY Philharmonic: Dudamel Conducts Mahler 5th + World Premiere
THU–MON SEP 24–29  ·  DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  UWS
Gustavo Dudamel's inaugural season as the New York Philharmonic's music and artistic director continues this week with Mahler's passionate Fifth Symphony — the soaring one, the one from Death in Venice. The program opens with a world premiere by Tania León, the Pulitzer Prize-winning Cuban-American composer. Dudamel is the biggest thing to happen to the Philharmonic in a generation; these early-season concerts will be talked about. Geffen Hall is steps from the apartment.
Getting in: Thursday Sep 24 and Friday Sep 25 evenings have good availability. Tickets from around $45. nyphil.org.

New York Film Festival opens Friday — NYFF64
OPENS FRI SEP 25  ·  LINCOLN CENTER + BAM & OTHERS  ·  THRU OCT 12  ·  $35/TICKET
The 64th New York Film Festival opens Friday night at Alice Tully Hall with Paper Tiger (distributed by Neon), and the weekend Spotlight Gala brings a world premiere: Godzilla Minus Zero, the sequel to the Oscar-winning Godzilla Minus One, screening Saturday night. General admission is $35 per screening with a two-ticket limit per film; pass holders and Film at Lincoln Center members got first access. Single tickets went on sale September 15 at noon — popular screenings are selling out but the full run through October 12 has options. The festival also screens at the Brooklyn Academy of Music and the Museum of the Moving Image in Astoria.
Getting in: Browse filmlinc.org for remaining availability; less-buzzed films in the main slate are the value play — same curators, same screens, no scramble.

Brooklyn Book Festival — Flagship Day
SUN SEP 27, 10AM–6PM  ·  BOROUGH HALL, DOWNTOWN BROOKLYN  ·  FREE
New York City's largest free literary festival runs all week (Sep 20–28) with events across all five boroughs, but the centerpiece is Sunday's Flagship Day in the open-air parks around Borough Hall — 250+ publishers, readings, panels, and a massive book market. This year's lineup includes Joyce Carol Oates, Marlon James (The Disappearers), Tracy K. Smith, Colson Whitehead, and Douglas Stuart; Amitav Ghosh is being honored as "Best of Brooklyn." A subway ride to Brooklyn for a literary afternoon followed by dinner in the neighborhood is an easy move if the weather breaks (Saturday clears somewhat, Sunday's forecast is still uncertain).
