The Surge: An Ode to Sinéad O'Connor — Joyce Theater
SEP 16–27  ·  THE JOYCE THEATER, CHELSEA  ·  INDOOR  ·  OPENS THIS WEEK
The Joyce Theater — Chelsea's dedicated contemporary dance house — opens its fall season with this tribute to Sinéad O'Connor, choreographed by Sonya Tayeh, who won the Tony Award for choreography on Moulin Rouge!. Tayeh is among the most kinetically intense choreographers working today; this kind of emotional, rock-music-anchored piece is exactly her zone. Gets more visceral than a concert, more physical than a memorial. Runs through the 27th — go early in the run.

Return of the Buffalo: Tatanka Owe Akupi
FRI SEP 19, 5–7:30PM  ·  BOWLING GREEN → FOLEY SQUARE  ·  FREE  ·  OUTDOOR — 71° PERFECT
A herd of 50 life-size buffalo puppets — each manipulated by a performer — will walk the streets of Lower Manhattan from Bowling Green to Foley Square on Friday evening. The work is by Palestinian director Amir Nizar Zuabi, created in partnership with Lenape and Lakota tribal elders. It's a reckoning piece: the American bison was nearly exterminated in the 19th century, and its return is a story that runs through Native American conservation history. Completely free, no tickets. Friday forecast: 71°F and sunny — ideal. If weather goes sideways, the route is short enough to do anyway; the spectacle is the thing. Presented by St. Ann's Warehouse, the experimental theater company based in DUMBO, Brooklyn.

100th Annual Feast of San Gennaro
OPENS THU SEP 17, THRU SEP 27  ·  MULBERRY ST, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE ENTRY  ·  OUTDOOR — IDEAL WEATHER MON-FRI
The 100th edition of the Feast of San Gennaro — an 11-day street festival that takes over 11 blocks of Mulberry Street in Little Italy each September, with 300-plus food vendors, live music, and carnival atmosphere. This centennial edition brings a Napoli Expo with three acclaimed Neapolitan artists and the Grand Procession returns on September 26. Free to enter; you pay only for food and games. This week's beautiful weather (70s and sun through Friday) makes the early days ideal. Weekday afternoons are uncrowded; weekends draw over a million visitors across the run. A Solemn High Mass celebrating San Gennaro takes place Saturday September 19 at 2pm at the Shrine Church of the Most Precious Blood on Mulberry. Fun detour on any downtown day.
