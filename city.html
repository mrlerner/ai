OPENS THU SEP 17 — THRU SEP 27  ·  MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE TO WANDER
New York City's oldest and largest Italian-American street festival turns 100 this year. Opens tomorrow (September 17) and runs through September 27, 11am–11pm weekdays, midnight on weekends. The centennial procession is Saturday, September 26 at 2pm — worth timing a visit around. Arthur Jafa: "I Am Tony" — New Museum (opens Sep 24)
OPENS SEP 24 — THRU JAN 4, 2027  ·  NEW MUSEUM, BOWERY  ·  INDOOR
The largest-ever survey of Arthur Jafa — filmmaker, cinematographer, and one of the most significant artists working in America today — fills two floors of the recently expanded New Museum. Named in honor of jazz drummer Tony Williams, the show spans nearly four decades of work including Love is the Message, The Message is Death (2016) and several new premieres. Opens next Wednesday, September 24; plan a visit during the NYFF run for a clean double-bill cultural day downtown.
Indoor / rain-proof. Runs through January 4, 2027 — no rush, but the opening-week energy is worth catching.
