Central Park Fall Walk — Ramble & North Woods
FREE · OUTDOOR · 63–73°F ALL WEEK · EARLY COLOR STARTING NOW
This is the week to be outside. A solid run of 63–73°F sunny days with no rain through Saturday makes it ideal for walking Central Park before peak foliage hits in mid-to-late October. The Ramble (a 36-acre woodland in the park's heart, between 73rd and 79th streets) has winding paths that feel nothing like Manhattan, and early fall color has started among the oaks and red maples. The North Woods (97th–106th streets) is even quieter. Pair with a late lunch at Barney Greengrass or El Fish on the Upper West Side. Indoor fallback: The Met Museum's Costume Institute galleries (permanent installation) are a 10-minute walk along the park's east side.

BAM Next Wave: Hewa Rwanda
OCT 7–11 · BROOKLYN ACADEMY OF MUSIC, HARVEY THEATER · BROOKLYN
The first piece in the Brooklyn Academy of Music's 2026 Next Wave festival — the annual fall showcase of international avant-garde theater, dance, and music that's been running since 1983. Hewa Rwanda is a new performance work by Rwandan playwright and director Odile Gakire Katese, blending live music, dance, and testimony from Kigali. One of the rarest things on any New York stage this fall. Tickets typically $35–$65. The combination of this show and Lilia or Misi pasta in Williamsburg makes a full Brooklyn evening.
