New York Film Festival 64 — Opening Weekend
OPENS TONIGHT — THRU OCT 12 · ALICE TULLY HALL + WALTER READE, LINCOLN CENTER · INDOOR
Film at Lincoln Center's 64th New York Film Festival opened tonight with James Gray's Paper Tiger in 35mm — a New York crime drama starring Adam Driver, Miles Teller, and Scarlett Johansson. This weekend's headliners: the North American premiere of Takashi Yamazaki's Godzilla Minus Zero (Saturday Spotlight Gala, 6pm); the US debut of Marine Atlan's La Gradiva (Sunday, 5:45pm), a French debut feature about teenagers on a transformative Naples trip; and Carlos Reygadas's Heil Jupiter! (Saturday, 2:15pm), described by critics as a dreamlike astonishment. All screenings at Alice Tully Hall or the Walter Reade Theater on West 65th Street. The festival runs through October 12 — opening-weekend films sell fastest.

Met Opera: Verdi's Macbeth — Saturday Matinee
SAT SEP 26 AT 1PM · METROPOLITAN OPERA HOUSE, LINCOLN CENTER · FROM $33
The Metropolitan Opera opened its 2026–27 season three nights ago with this production of Verdi's Macbeth — soprano Lise Davidsen as Lady Macbeth, baritone Quinn Kelsey in the title role, conducted by Met Music Director Yannick Nézet-Séguin — and the combination is drawing serious attention. Saturday's 1pm matinee is the ideal rainy-afternoon play: the Met is grand, heated, and 2½ hours of this cast is not remotely a hardship. Family Circle balcony seats from $33; orchestra higher. (Saturday evening also has Mozart's Così fan tutte if a second opera night sounds right.) The Macbeth run continues through October 20.

NY Philharmonic: Dudamel Conducts Mahler — Last 3 Performances
SAT–MON SEP 26–29 · DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER · INDOOR · 10 MIN FROM UPPER WEST SIDE
Gustavo Dudamel, the Venezuelan conductor in his much-anticipated inaugural season as the New York Philharmonic's music director, leads Mahler's impassioned Fifth Symphony alongside a world premiere by the Cuban-American composer Tania León. This program runs only Saturday, Sunday, and Monday before it closes. David Geffen Hall is a ten-minute walk from the Upper West Side. One of the cultural events of the fall; 90 minutes, fully indoor, and the Dudamel-Philharmonic pairing is generating genuine momentum this season.

Fall for Dance: Program 3 — The Royal Ballet
SAT SEP 26 + SUN SEP 27 · NEW YORK CITY CENTER, W. 55TH ST · ALL SEATS $30
New York City Center's annual fall dance festival — every ticket priced at $30 regardless of seat — presents Program 3 this weekend, headlined by the Royal Ballet of London alongside Spanish contemporary dance artist Juan Tomás de la Molía and the Israeli Lior Tavori Dance Company. Three distinct companies in one evening: that's the festival's proposition, a sampler of what world-class companies are making right now. The festival continues through October 3, with Program 4 (San Francisco Ballet) next week.

KRAMER/FAUCI — Opens Sunday at St. Ann's Warehouse
OPENS SUN SEP 27 — THRU OCT 24 · ST. ANN'S WAREHOUSE, DUMBO, BROOKLYN · INDOOR
Daniel Fish — the director who deconstructed Oklahoma! into a dark meditation on community violence and turned it into one of the defining Broadway moments of 2019 — opens a new play about the long, contentious, world-changing relationship between AIDS activist Larry Kramer and Dr. Anthony Fauci. Fish's productions tend to reframe material you think you know into something you've never seen before. St. Ann's Warehouse, housed in a former tobacco warehouse in the DUMBO neighborhood of Brooklyn, is one of New York's most adventurous presenters. Opening night is Sunday; the run goes through October 24.

Arthur Jafa: I Am Tony — New Museum
OPENED YESTERDAY — THRU EARLY 2027 · NEW MUSEUM, 235 BOWERY · WED–SUN · INDOOR
The largest museum survey ever mounted for Arthur Jafa — the filmmaker and visual artist whose 2016 video work Love Is the Message, the Message Is Death (seven minutes of Black American life set to Kanye West) won the Golden Lion at the Venice Biennale in 2019 — opened yesterday at the New Museum on the Bowery. The show, titled I Am Tony, spans his entire career. The New Museum is open Wednesday through Sunday; this weekend is the first public opportunity to see it. An hour here pairs naturally with a walk to the Lower East Side or dinner downtown.
