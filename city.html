NY Phil: Sounds of Remembrance — 9/11 at 25
FRI SEP 11 · 3:30PM · PERELMAN PERFORMING ARTS CENTER · LOWER MANHATTAN · FREE
A landmark free concert on the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks, steps from the site at the Perelman Performing Arts Center (PAC NYC) in Lower Manhattan. Dudamel conducts the Philharmonic alongside the Soldiers' Chorus of the U.S. Army Field Band.
