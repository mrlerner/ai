"You Should Be Dancing: New York, 1976 and Beyond"
OPENS FRI OCT 2 · NEW-YORK HISTORICAL SOCIETY, CENTRAL PARK WEST · THROUGH SPRING 2027

Told through artifacts, photography, and ephemera. The New-York Historical Society is the museum at 77th and Central Park West — UWS home turf, a short walk from the apartment.
