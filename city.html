New York City Ballet — Masters at Work I
TUE SEP 29 + WED SEP 30  ·  DAVID H. KOCH THEATER AT LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  UWS HOME TURF
After Jewels closes today, New York City Ballet shifts into mixed repertory mode with "Masters at Work I" — three Balanchine pieces from the purest period of 20th-century American ballet. The anchor is The Four Temperaments, Balanchine's 1946 masterwork set to a Paul Hindemith score commissioned from him personally. The Concert (or, The Perils of Everybody) is comedic and beloved — umbrellas, sleeping men, and marital chaos set to Chopin. The David H. Koch Theater is the anchor of Lincoln Center's west plaza, a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side. Clear Tuesday evening, buy on the day.

Arthur Jafa: "I Am Tony" — New Museum
JUST OPENED SEP 24 — THROUGH JAN 4, 2027  ·  NEW MUSEUM, 235 BOWERY  ·  LOWER EAST SIDE  ·  INDOOR
The largest survey ever mounted of filmmaker and multimedia artist Arthur Jafa — nearly four decades of work across two floors of the New Museum on the Bowery. The anchor is his canonical video work Love is the Message, The Message is Death (2016), a 7-minute film set to Kanye West's "Ultralight Beam" that assembles footage of Black American life — joy, violence, athleticism, spirituality — into something that hits harder than almost any other art made in this decade. Alongside it: The White Album, newly premiered pieces, and photography. Best on a clear weekday afternoon (Tuesday or Wednesday) when the crowds are thin. General admission around $20.

Met Opera: Verdi's Macbeth
RUNS THROUGH OCT 20  ·  METROPOLITAN OPERA HOUSE, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR  ·  UWS HOME TURF
The 2026–27 season opener, now three weeks into its run. Norwegian soprano Lise Davidsen — the most talked-about dramatic soprano of her generation — plays Lady Macbeth opposite baritone Quinn Kelsey as Macbeth, with Yannick Nézet-Séguin conducting. Verdi's Macbeth is shorter and leaner than most of his operas (under 3 hours), making it a good entry point. Mid-week evenings (Wednesday or Thursday this week) are the most comfortable for a last-minute ticket. The Met's standing-room tickets at $25 go on sale at 10am on performance day.
