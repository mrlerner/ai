New York Philharmonic: Mahler's Fifth & a Tania León World Premiere
SAT 7:30PM · SUN 2PM · TUE 7:30PM  ·  DAVID GEFFEN HALL AT LINCOLN CENTER  ·  FROM $288
These are the final three performances of Gustavo Dudamel's first signature program at Geffen Hall — his home stage since taking over as music director in September. The Pulitzer Prize–winning Cuban-American composer Tania León gets a world premiere alongside Mahler's monumental Fifth Symphony. David Geffen Hall is a ten-minute walk from the Upper West Side. Today and Sunday are perfect excuses to be inside one of the best concert halls in the world; the Tuesday night show carries a slightly lower price and still-open seating.
Rain-day call: The nor'easter makes this the move for Saturday night. Sunday's 2pm matinee is ideal if Saturday feels too late to schlep in the storm.

KRAMER/FAUCI — Opens Sunday at St. Ann's Warehouse
OPENS SUN SEP 27  ·  ST. ANN'S WAREHOUSE, DUMBO, BROOKLYN  ·  GET TICKETS EARLY
Daniel Fish — the director who turned Oklahoma! into a psychological thriller — opens a new play examining the volatile relationship between AIDS activist Larry Kramer and public-health official Anthony Fauci during the AIDS epidemic. St. Ann's Warehouse in Brooklyn's Dumbo neighborhood is where major international theater productions get their US premieres; this one is brand new. Fish's productions tend to sell out fast — worth buying before reviews land and prices jump. Natural pairing with dinner at Lilia or Misi (Williamsburg, 10 min away) for the full evening.

Arthur Jafa: "I Am Tony" — New Museum
OPENED SEP 24  ·  NEW MUSEUM, 235 BOWERY, LOWER EAST SIDE  ·  THROUGH WINTER
The largest-ever survey of Arthur Jafa — the filmmaker and visual artist best known for the six-minute video work "Love Is the Message, the Message Is Death" (2016), which layers Black American music and imagery over footage of Black life in a way that stops people cold. This is his first full US retrospective. Opened Thursday; the nor'easter makes today a natural museum day. The New Museum building on the Bowery is one of the best gallery spaces in the city.
