Central Park: Rowboats + Ramble Walk
TODAY–MON LABOR DAY  ·  FREE (WALK) / $20/HR (ROWBOATS)  ·  OUTDOOR — IDEAL CONDITIONS ALL WEEKEND
79°F today, 75°F Sunday, 78°F Labor Day Monday — all sunny, nearly zero rain. This is the best outdoor window of the fall. The rowboat rental dock at Loeb Boathouse opens at 10am; $20/hour plus deposit. For the afternoon, the Ramble (a 36-acre wooded section in the center of the park, two minutes from the Upper West Side) is at peak late-summer mood — shaded paths, the lake, birding. Pair with a Tatiana bar walk-in attempt (6 seats, no reservation) or dinner at Cafe Luxembourg nearby. Indoor fallback if weather shifts: the American Museum of Natural History and its new Gilder Center are a 10-minute walk from the park's west edge.

Chelsea Fall Gallery Openings — Thursday Night
THU SEP 10, 6–8PM  ·  CHELSEA ART DISTRICT (WEST 20s–25s)  ·  FREE
September is when the Chelsea gallery world reconvenes — 20+ galleries on West 22nd–25th Streets launch their fall shows the same Thursday evening, and doors are open to anyone. No tickets, no RSVPs. It's one of the few genuinely free cultural nights in Manhattan where you can walk from room to room and stumble into something remarkable. Artforum's fall 2026 preview names the headliner shows; expect wine, crowds, and conversation. Worth pairing with dinner at Graciela or Cleo (West Village, 10 minutes south) afterward.
