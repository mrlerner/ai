Tribute in Light — 25th Anniversary
TONIGHT, DUSK THROUGH DAWN  ·  LOWER MANHATTAN SKY  ·  FREE  ·  VISIBLE CITYWIDE
Two columns of light made from 88 xenon bulbs rise over Lower Manhattan tonight — the annual remembrance of the towers. The 25th is a significant one. Visible from a clear rooftop, the Brooklyn Bridge, or anywhere with a south-facing sightline. No ticket, no reservation — just go look.

NY Phil: Dudamel at Geffen Hall — "On the Transmigration of Souls"
TUE–FRI SEP 16–19  ·  DAVID GEFFEN HALL, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  FROM ~$40
Gustavo Dudamel's first concerts at Geffen Hall as Music & Artistic Director of the New York Philharmonic — his real home after the Radio City opener. The centerpiece is John Adams's Pulitzer Prize–winning On the Transmigration of Souls, which the Philharmonic co-commissioned and premiered in 2002 to honor the victims of September 11. Hearing it this week, 25 years later, with Dudamel conducting, is the kind of thing you don't get twice. Also on the program: a world premiere by Zosha Di Castri and Prokofiev's Symphony No. 5. Lincoln Center is a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side.

100th Feast of San Gennaro — Little Italy
OPENS THU SEP 17, THROUGH SUN SEP 27  ·  MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE ENTRY
The 100th running of New York City's oldest and biggest Italian street festival — 11 days on Mulberry Street between Canal and Houston, 300-plus food and vendor stalls, live music, and a solemn procession honoring the patron saint of Naples on September 26. The centennial year makes this one worth the trip downtown. Hours: 11:30am–11:30pm daily (midnight on Friday and Saturday). The forecast for Thursday is sunny at 79° — good timing to hit it on opening day before the crowds build.

Illumination 2026 — Light Art Festival
THU–SAT SEP 17–19  ·  WAGNER PARK, BATTERY PARK AREA  ·  7–11PM  ·  FREE
The fifth annual outdoor light art festival along the Hudson at Wagner Park — a quiet, design-oriented walk through large-scale illuminated installations with Lower Manhattan as backdrop. Better than it sounds: this is the understated, adults-only version of a light festival, not the Vegas-in-a-park kind. Thursday evening forecast is partly cloudy at 70° — pair it with the Feast of San Gennaro nearby for a full downtown evening.
