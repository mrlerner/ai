THU OCT 8 AT 7:30PM  ·  SUN OCT 11 AT 3PM  ·  METROPOLITAN OPERA HOUSE, LINCOLN CENTER  ·  INDOOR
Verdi's Macbeth — not Shakespeare's play but Verdi's operatic transformation of it — with Norwegian soprano Lise Davidsen as Lady Macbeth. Davidsen is arguably the biggest name in opera right now: a voice the size of a building and one of the great Lady Macbeths of the current era. Conductor Yannick Nézet-Séguin leads. CLOSES SUNDAY OCT 11  ·  BAM HARVEY THEATER, BROOKLYN  ·  BAM NEXT WAVE FESTIVAL  ·  INDOOR

Limón Dance Company at The Joyce Theater
THROUGH SUNDAY OCT 11  ·  THE JOYCE THEATER, 175 EIGHTH AVE, CHELSEA  ·  INDOOR  ·  CLOSES THIS WEEK
Founded in 1947 by Mexican-American choreographer José Limón, the Limón Dance Company is one of the oldest continually performing modern dance companies in the United States — a living link to the founders of American modern dance. The Joyce Theater in Chelsea is the city's dedicated contemporary dance house: 470 seats, close-up sight lines, and an intimate connection to the performers. Pairing: West Village dinner afterward — Graciela, Capitaine, or Via Carota walk-in are all within a 10-minute walk.

Central Park Ramble + North Woods — Peak Foliage Walk
ALL WEEK THROUGH SAT  ·  CENTRAL PARK, UPPER WEST SIDE  ·  FREE  ·  OUTDOOR — SKIP SUNDAY (21% RAIN AFTER 2PM)
Five straight sunny days, 65–75°F, with mid-October peak foliage in Central Park. The Ramble (a 36-acre wild woodland in the middle of the park, just above 72nd Street) and the North Woods (the wilder, quieter northern reaches above 102nd Street) are the best foliage spots in the park — both a short walk from the Upper West Side apartment. Warmest window: Thursday (75°F). Standard pairing: Barney Greengrass (the 100-year-old smoked-fish institution on Amsterdam Ave, walk-in cash only, weekends have a line) for brunch before the walk, or Smoke Jazz Club (Upper West Side jazz club with dinner seating) in the evening after.
Indoor fallback (rain or rest day): The Met Museum just opened a major new show — Lee Krasner and Jackson Pollock: Past Continuous — which pairs the two Abstract Expressionist giants and runs through the winter. Five-minute walk from the park's east side. Café Sabarsky at the Neue Galerie (Upper East Side, 86th and Fifth) is the other weather-proof ace: Viennese café in a mansion, worth it any day.
