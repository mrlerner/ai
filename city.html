New York Philharmonic: Gustavo Dudamel Inaugural
THU SEP 10 · 7:30PM  ·  RADIO CITY MUSIC HALL, MIDTOWN  ·  INDOOR
Thursday night, Gustavo Dudamel — the Venezuelan conductor who is the world's most celebrated music director — officially takes the podium as the New York Philharmonic's new Tang Music and Artistic Director. He's opening at Radio City Music Hall (not the orchestra's home at Geffen Hall, which is a 10-minute walk from the Upper West Side) with a gala program that includes works by Philip Glass and Jessie Montgomery, plus special guests Lizzo, St. Vincent, and Joaquina. This is a cultural timestamp — the kind of opening night that gets written about for years. Check nyphil.org now; remaining tickets are going fast.

Labor Day in Central Park — Last Summer Monday
MON SEP 7  ·  ALL DAY  ·  UPPER WEST SIDE  ·  78°F SUNNY, 0% RAIN
The forecast is perfect and it's a holiday: rowboats on the Lake at the Loeb Boathouse ($20/hr, no advance booking needed, show up and wait if there's a line), then through the Ramble — a 36-acre forested maze in the middle of Manhattan that most tourists never find. Pick up smoked fish and bagels from Barney Greengrass (480 Amsterdam Ave, cash, worth the line) on the way in. If the park is too crowded, pivot to Little Island on the Hudson River — free entry, less foot traffic, better views. Indoor fallback if the morning rain lingers: Neue Galerie (Klimt, Schiele, Café Sabarsky for Viennese coffee) at 86th and 5th, a 15-minute walk.
