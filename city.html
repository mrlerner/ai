Return of the Buffalo: Tatanka Owe Akupi
FRI SEP 18 (DOWNTOWN BROOKLYN) + SAT SEP 19, 5–7:30PM (LOWER MANHATTAN)  ·  FREE  ·  OUTDOOR
Fifty life-size buffalo puppets — each operated by a team of puppeteers — parade through city streets, joined by gospel choirs, Lenape and Lakota elders, dancers, and marching bands. The St. Ann's Warehouse production travels Downtown Brooklyn on Friday, then roams Lower Manhattan on Saturday (Bowling Green to Foley Square). Created in collaboration with Indigenous tribal elders; conceived by director Amir Nizar Zuabi. Free, no tickets. Saturday's crisp 69° sunshine is perfect for following the herd; indoor fallback: head to the nearby Frick Madison or the New-York Historical Society if weather shifts.

Feast of San Gennaro — 100th Year
THROUGH SEP 27  ·  MULBERRY ST, LITTLE ITALY  ·  FREE ADMISSION  ·  11:30AM–11:30PM DAILY
The century mark. Eleven blocks of Mulberry Street (between Canal and Houston) taken over by vendors selling sausage and peppers, fried calamari, cannoli, and riceballs. Saturday September 19 is the grand procession day — parade from Most Precious Blood Church at 2pm — which makes it the peak day to go, and it lines up perfectly with the buffalo herd's Manhattan leg (do both). Completely walkable from a cab or train at Canal. No rain plan needed: street festivals survive drizzle, and this one runs daily regardless.
