TODAY + TOMORROW · DOWNTOWN BROOKLYN & LOWER MANHATTAN · FREE

Met Opera Season Opener: Verdi's Macbeth
TUE SEP 22 + RUNS THROUGH OCT 20 · MET OPERA HOUSE, LINCOLN CENTER · UPPER WEST SIDE
The Metropolitan Opera's 2026–27 season opens with a new production of Verdi's Macbeth — baritone Quinn Kelsey in the title role, soprano Lise Davidsen as Lady Macbeth, and Music Director Yannick Nézet-Séguin on the podium. Davidsen, the Norwegian soprano who owns the Wagner and Strauss repertoire, takes on early Verdi for the first time here, which is a programming gamble worth watching. Opening night is the gala occasion; later performances through October 20 are an easier ticket. The October 17 performance is broadcast to cinemas worldwide as part of the Metropolitan Opera's Live in HD series — worth noting as an easy entry point. Tickets at metopera.org.

Feast of San Gennaro — 100th Anniversary
THROUGH SUN SEP 27 · MULBERRY STREET, LITTLE ITALY · FREE TO WALK
New York City's oldest and largest Italian-American street festival — a century old this year — runs along Mulberry Street in Little Italy through September 27. Free to walk, with carnival rides, religious processions, and a dense corridor of vendors selling sausage and peppers, zeppole, and cannoli. Open from 11:30am daily; Friday and Saturday nights go to midnight. A low-stakes, high-color way to spend an afternoon, and this year's centennial edition is a slightly bigger deal than usual. Works in the rain; just plan around the Saturday-Sunday crowds.
