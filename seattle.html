SATURDAY DAYTIME · NATURE, NEW · FREE
Cedar River Salmon Journey: Seattle Aquarium naturalists at the spawning run
SAT OCT 3, 10AM–2PM  ·  FREE, DROP IN  ·  RENTON LIBRARY (100 MILL AVE S, THE LIBRARY BUILT OVER THE RIVER) AND CEDAR RIVER PARK (BEHIND THE RENTON COMMUNITY CENTER)  ·  ALSO SUN OCT 4, 11–2, CEDAR GRANGE, MAPLE VALLEY
Found on EverOut’s cheap-weekend list and verified on the aquarium’s program page this morning. The Cedar is the Lake Washington system’s big sockeye river, and this is the watching side of your salmon life, not the fishing side: aquarium naturalists stand at the bank with the spawning fish below, explaining the color change, the hooked jaws, and which species are in (the page names Chinook, sockeye and coho). The Renton Library is the easy stop, because the building spans the river and the viewing is from the walkway under your feet; Cedar River Park is a short walk upstream. Both run 10 to 2 tomorrow only, then the program moves upriver (Riverview Park and Belmondo Reach Oct 17 and 24; Cedar Grange in Maple Valley Sun Oct 4, 11–2). Forecast: 26% cloud, 0% rain, 61–63° at noon.

Then, on the way home: the Fishermen’s Fall Festival at Fishermen’s Terminal, 11am–6pm, free (the memorial foundation’s page still says “October 3, 2026”): the North Pacific fleet home at the docks, boat tours, the survival-suit race, chowder, proceeds to the families of fishers lost at sea. 1900 W Nickerson. The page sends you to Facebook for the hour-by-hour schedule. Other Saturday lines: U District Chow Down, 11–8, $6 bites from 70-odd businesses on a closed-off Ave; the free Shoreline Music Summit at Shoreline Community College, 11–9, with an hour of songs from Tomo Nakayama and Alie Byland (times not published; free RSVP via Eventbrite); the Northwest Chocolate Festival at Seattle Center, Sat 9–6, EverOut quotes $20–150.

The site’s own line: “be prepared to hike a half mile or so to reach the orchard,” and it recommends the Viewlands Elementary trail “if you are agile.” Piper’s Creek, downhill, gets its own chum run in November; this is the walk to scout it.
