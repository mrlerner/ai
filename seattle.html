The History of Concrete (John Wilson, 2026)
TUE OCT 6, 7PM  ·  101 MIN  ·  $15 GENERAL / $10 STUDENT / $7 MEMBER  ·  NORTHWEST FILM FORUM, 1515 12TH AVE, CAPITOL HILL
The urbanism pick of the week, and the one that ends earliest. John Wilson, who made How To with John Wilson for HBO (deadpan narration over thousands of shots of New York sidewalks), has made a feature about the material the city is literally poured from; the Film Forum’s own line is that he looks to the bedrock of his beloved New York City for inspiration and structure. Expect the same method: a simple question (what is concrete, who mixes it, why does it crack) that wanders into the people who hold cities up. Last Seattle showings are tonight, Mon Oct 12 at 6:30 and Tue Oct 13 at 6:30, so this is the first of three chances, and the evening sky is going grey anyway (52% cloud by 7). 101 minutes: seated 7, out 8:45, home by 9.
