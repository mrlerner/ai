Last big minus tides of summer — Mon, Tue, Wed mornings
MON −2.4 FT ~9–11AM  ·  TUE −2.6 FT  ·  WED −2.5 FT  ·  FREE
Three straight mornings of serious minus tides under sunny skies — the last naturalist-staffed lows of the season. Environmental Science Center naturalists are on Seahurst Beach in Burien (Mon 9–11am, Tue 9:30–11:30am, Wed 10am–noon), or go self-guided closer to home at Golden Gardens or Carkeek — moon snails, anemones, and acres of exposed flats. After Wednesday the daytime lows are gone until next summer.

Duck Dodge sailboat race — Tuesday evening, Lake Union
TUE AUG 11, EVENING  ·  FREE TO WATCH  ·  SUNNY, 77°
Seattle's 52-year-old anything-goes Tuesday-night sailboat race, in what should be perfect calm-and-sunny conditions. The best seat isn't on land: put a kayak in at Agua Verde or Northwest Outdoor Center and drift with the spectator fleet mid-lake. Land option: the Gas Works Park lawn with takeout. Races run most Tuesdays through early September, but this is the best forecast in weeks.

SAT AUG 15 & SUN AUG 16, 10AM–7PM  ·  FREE  ·  SUNNY, 81°
