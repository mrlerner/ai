Freddy Fuego’s 15-piece orchestra: the jazz festival’s last matinee
SUN OCT 25  ·  2PM  ·  $44  ·  TOWN HALL SEATTLE, 1119 8TH AVE, FIRST HILL
A trombonist and composer bringing a 15-piece orchestra to Town Hall to launch his new album, Moment of Truth; Town Hall’s write-up puts it where jazz, hip-hop and classical meet. Bring a red light, step around the anemones, and leave everything where it lies. Indoor fallback: none needed, the tide repeats nightly through Saturday.
