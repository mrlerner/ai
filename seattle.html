Big Star Quintet: the Memphis band behind Teenage Fanclub, played by its heirs
WED NOV 4  ·  DOORS 7, SHOW 8, NO END TIME PUBLISHED  ·  ALL AGES, GENERAL ADMISSION  ·  NEPTUNE THEATRE, UNIVERSITY DISTRICT
Big Star is the 1970s Memphis power-pop band nearly every jangly guitar group of the 90s learned from; Teenage Fanclub, on your recent-discoveries list, basically built a career on them. This touring quintet is billed with Mike Mills of R.E.M., Chris Stamey of the dB’s and Jon Auer of the Posies (on earlier legs, drummer Jody Stephens, the last original member, and Pat Sansone of Wilco), playing the songs, recently the whole Radio City album. Listen first: “September Gurls,” “Thirteen.” Late for you (8pm start), but a once-around-the-country kind of booking. Price is only on Ticketmaster; the venue page doesn’t say it’s sold out.

Chum salmon in Piper’s Creek, the morning after the rain
SAT & SUN 11AM–3PM, WED 2–4PM, THROUGH DEC 6  ·  FREE  ·  CARKEEK PARK, NORTHWEST SEATTLE
Today’s rain is the last push the creek gets before a dry week, and volunteer Salmon Stewards are on the bank weekends 11–3 to point out fish. Sunday is the day: partly sunny, 1% rain, high 54, and the clock change gives you an extra hour of morning. Saturday works too (2%). Stay on trails, out of the creek bed. Peak spawning is usually around Thanksgiving; this is an early look.
