TONIGHT · GREY DAY, STAY IN · FREE · BRANCH-OUT MUSIC
What’s Going On, night one: Wayne Horvitz conducts, then a trio
WED NOV 18, 7:30PM (DOORS 6:30)  ·  FREE, BY DONATION, RSVP  ·  THE ROYAL ROOM, COLUMBIA CITY
New to the brief this morning. The opening night of a four-night festival at the Royal Room, the small Columbia City jazz room, devoted this year to the 1980s New York “downtown” scene, where free jazz, punk and experimental composition crossed (John Zorn, Henry Threadgill, Butch Morris). Set one is a conduction workshop: Wayne Horvitz, the Seattle composer, and Stephanie Richards lead a group of local improvisers with hand signals, and the public can watch or bring an instrument and join. Set two is a trio of Ayesha Brooks, Leanna Keith and Ha-Yang Kim playing Morris, Zorn, Threadgill and Horvitz. Branch-out read: closer to the Steve Reich and minimalism end of your listening than to anything with a chorus, and you get to see how an improvised orchestra is steered (that comparison is mine). It is free; the ticket page asks for an online RSVP, and seats go first come unless you book dinner. Two sets from 7:30 with no end time printed, so likely past 9. The rest of the festival is ticketed: Friday and Saturday nights cover “New York in the 80s,” with guests including the guitarist Fred Frith.
