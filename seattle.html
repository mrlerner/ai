TONIGHT OR WEDNESDAY · NEW · BRANCH-OUT MUSIC
Two jazz guitarists, no band: Frank Vignola and Pasquale Grasso
TUE NOV 10 & WED NOV 11, 7:30PM (DOORS 6)  ·  $41 INCLUDING FEES  ·  DIMITRIOU’S JAZZ ALLEY, DOWNTOWN
This week’s branch-out, confirmed on the club’s own page after two runs of conflicting listings. Vignola is the swing-and-songbook virtuoso; Grasso is the Italian-born player who works bebop piano lines (Bud Powell is his model) onto six strings, and has backed singer Samara Joy. If you like watching fingerpickers up close, this is the jazz end of that: two guitars, every seat within 50 feet of the stage, assigned seating, dinner optional. A 7:30 set at a seated dinner club; the page gives no end time. Tonight is the rain night (99% by day), tomorrow lets you pair it with a dry day. Earlier and free today: Allison Russell’s in-studio at KEXP, Seattle Center, 3–3:30pm, still listed as open to the public this morning. Also tonight: É.T.É, a Quebec trio of fiddle, cello, bouzouki and step-dance, Royal Room, 7:30, $20/$25; and the last screening of Varda’s Vagabond, Northwest Film Forum, 7pm, $15.

NEXT TUESDAY · MUSIC
The Naghash Ensemble of Armenia
TUE NOV 17, 7:30PM  ·  $43  ·  TOWN HALL, FIRST HILL
Now inside the week. Armenian folk and sacred song rewritten as contemporary composition, presented by SAMA: Music + Art. FRI NOV 20 · NEW · URBANISM
Transportation and the Shape of Cities
FRI NOV 20, 7PM  ·  $10  ·  WYNCOTE NORTHWEST FORUM, TOWN HALL, FIRST HILL
Ten days out, but squarely your subject and cheap. The authors of a new book of the same name present a framework for transportation design across every urban mode (walking, micromobility, cars, trains, transit), built on geometry, networks and urban design and pitched in plain language. Then a panel moderated by Kirk Hovenkotter and audience questions. It is in the smaller downstairs room, so buy ahead.
