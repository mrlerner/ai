The Stories Water Keeps opens at noon today on Pier 66
SAT NOV 21, 12–8PM · SUN NOV 22, 10AM–6PM  ·  FREE, RSVP REQUESTED  ·  BAY AUDITORIUM, PIER 66
It starts today: a free weekend of films and panels on the histories of the Seattle waterfront, in an auditorium over the water. Schedule read on the organizer’s page this morning. Today: doors at noon; Tugboat Annie, the 1933 comedy, at 1pm with a historians’ panel at 2:30; The Harbor Island Scheme, a short with its creator, at 3:40; a Suquamish maritime film at 4:30 with a panel at 4:40; Fish War, on the treaty fishing-rights fight, at 5:50 with a Salmon Homecoming Alliance panel at 7:10. The morning and early afternoon are dry (0–7% rain through 3pm), so walk the waterfront first and be inside by the time rain chances reach 31% at 5pm. Sunday is still the one for you: Stories From the Salish Sea at 12:20pm, then at 12:40 a panel with the orca researcher Deborah Giles and her scent-detection dog Eba (Eba meets the public in the lobby at 1:30); The River Remembers at 1:50 with a panel at 3; Ebb and Flow at 3:50. Ivar’s sells food on site. A free firepit social runs 3 to 6pm both days next door on Pier 62, weather permitting; Sunday’s looks good at 2% rain.

Paul Loeb: how people kept going against long odds
