TODAY · BRANCH-OUT OF THE WEEK · 2PM · SUNNY, 58°
Grupo Falso Baiano: Brazilian choro on a sunny Sunday afternoon
SUN OCT 18  ·  2–4PM  ·  $20 ADVANCE / $25 DOOR, ALL AGES  ·  THE ROYAL ROOM, 5000 RAINIER AVE S, COLUMBIA CITY
Last call on this one: it was still on the Royal Room calendar this morning, 2 to 4pm. Choro is Rio’s turn-of-the-century acoustic instrumental music, quick, interlocking melodies on guitar, the small cavaquinho and winds. Sounds like a string band playing chamber jazz. Sits next to Hermanos Gutiérrez and Steve Gunn’s fingerpicking. Out at 4 with over two hours of sun left: walk it off along Lake Washington at Genesee Park or Seward Park, ten minutes east.
Free alternative downtown: the Benaroya Hall Open House, from 9am (schedule as read Oct 14): Steve Winter’s National Geographic big-cats talk at 11, the Seattle Repertory Jazz Orchestra at 2, and the Seattle Symphony with The Head and the Heart at 4 (free tickets handed out in person 9am–1pm, two per person). Then the waterfront is two blocks downhill.

MONDAY EVENING · IDEAS · NEW · 7:30PM
Barbara Kingsolver on Partita, her new novel about a pianist
MON OCT 19  ·  7:30PM  ·  $10–126  ·  SEATTLE ARTS & LECTURES, BENAROYA HALL (TAPER AUDITORIUM), 200 UNIVERSITY ST, DOWNTOWN
New to the brief (spotted on EverOut’s talks rail; confirmed on the Seattle Arts & Lectures page). The Pulitzer winner behind Demon Copperhead talks about Partita, out Oct 6: a woman in farm country, once a gifted conservatory pianist, gets a call from the old lover who derailed her, and the past unspools in chapters framed as musical dances. For a solo-piano listener, it’s the rare literary night that’s actually about music. Seattle Arts & Lectures publishes no run time; figure out around 9.
Earlier, and shorter: danah boyd with former Census Bureau acting director Ron Jarmin on how the 2020 census got counted, Town Hall, 6pm, $10–35, out by about 7:15. You could do both.

SAT OCT 24  ·  5PM  ·  $44 INCLUDING FEES  ·  THE ROYAL ROOM, 5000 RAINIER AVE S, COLUMBIA CITY
Moved up from the calendar now that it’s six days out.
