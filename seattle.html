SAT OR SUN · NATURE · 11AM–3PM · FREE · DRY WEEKEND
Chum salmon in Piper’s Creek, with the stewards on the bank
SAT & SUN 11AM–3PM, WED 2–4PM, THROUGH DEC 6  ·  FREE  ·  CARKEEK PARK, NORTHWEST SEATTLE
New this week. Seattle Parks says the chum run in Piper’s Creek started last weekend, and trained volunteer Salmon Stewards are on the creek Saturdays and Sundays 11–3 (plus Wednesdays 2–4) through December 6 to explain what the fish are doing; they also point out the beavers working the wetland. The stewards were hoping for rain to pull fish upstream, and they’re getting three days of it, so the first dry weekend after is good timing. Go Saturday: 11–3 is 8% rain on the hourly grid. Stay on the trails, out of the creek bed. Spawning usually peaks around Thanksgiving, so this is the early look; come back then. If the weekend turns wet: today’s 2–4 steward shift, in showers.
