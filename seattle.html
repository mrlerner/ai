THU · MUSIC · BRANCH-OUT · 7:30PM · $20 ADVANCE
Akira Tana and Otonowa: Japanese folk songs, rebuilt as jazz
THU NOV 5, DOORS 6:30, SHOW 7:30  ·  $20 ADVANCE / $25 DOOR  ·  ROYAL ROOM, COLUMBIA CITY
This week’s branch-out. Drummer Akira Tana started Otonowa (“sound circle”) as a benefit for northern Japan after the 2011 tsunami; the band re-harmonizes traditional Japanese folk and pop melodies for a jazz group, with shakuhachi flute and taiko drums alongside. Sounds like a gentle modal jazz record with a bamboo flute carrying the tune; sits next to the solo-piano and minimalist things you stream, not the bebop end. Advance tickets online only; seating is first come.
Same night: The Tallest Man on Earth, the Swedish fingerpicker (next to Nick Drake), the Crocodile, 8pm, 21+; also listed sold out on the venue calendar. Thursday daytime is the dry gap (21%): free admission all day at the National Nordic Museum in Ballard, $5 for the new Salmon Bay shipyard exhibit.
