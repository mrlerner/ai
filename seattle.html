Arctic ice, Salish Sea fishermen, and who looks after the water
FRI NOV 13, 7:30PM (DOORS 6:30, OUT ~8:45)  ·  $10–35 SLIDING SCALE  ·  TOWN HALL, FIRST HILL
Braided River, a conservation-photography publisher, hosts an evening on Indigenous knowledge and conservation. Kiliii Yüyan, a National Geographic Explorer of Chinese and Nanai descent, shows work from Arctic sea ice and underwater; with him is Jay Julius, a fisherman and former Lummi Nation chairman who now leads Se’Si’Le, a Bellingham Indigenous nonprofit. Ocean photography plus a working Salish Sea fisherman’s view of salmon and the shoreline — squarely your water lane. A standard 75-minute Town Hall talk.
