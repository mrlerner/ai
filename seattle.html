Use the sun: Ballard’s fishing fleet, then the ship canal
THU NOV 5, 10AM–8PM  ·  FREE (SHIPYARD EXHIBIT $5)  ·  NATIONAL NORDIC MUSEUM, BALLARD
First Thursday means free general admission at the Nordic Museum; the new exhibit on Pacific Fishermen, the Salmon Bay shipyard that has kept the North Pacific fleet running for a century, is $5 extra. Pair it with a walk along the canal to Fishermen’s Terminal or the Ballard Locks while it’s still 4% rain; after today the next dry stretch isn’t in the forecast. Also tonight in Pioneer Square: the First Thursday Art Walk, galleries open into the evening.
TONIGHT · MUSIC · BRANCH-OUT · 7:30PM · $20–25
Shakuhachi flute and taiko over a jazz rhythm section
THU NOV 5, DOORS 6:30, SHOW 7:30  ·  $20 ADVANCE / $25 DOOR  ·  ROYAL ROOM, COLUMBIA CITY
Still on the Royal Room calendar this morning. Drummer Akira Tana’s Otonowa takes Japanese folk and pop melodies and re-harmonizes them for a jazz group, with bamboo flute and taiko in the mix. The branch-out of the week: unhurried modal jazz where a flute sings the tune, nearer the solo piano you stream than to bebop.
Or food and laughs: Nigel Ng, the comedian behind the Uncle Roger cooking videos, with Kristi Brown, on his cookbook Don’t Mess Up Your Rice: wok technique, rice cookers and why MSG is king. Town Hall, 7:30, $10–35, about 75 minutes. Free, same hour: Valeria Luiselli, the Mexican novelist, also at Town Hall. The Tallest Man on Earth at the Crocodile is sold out.
