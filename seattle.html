NEW EXHIBIT · NATURE · OPEN TODAY
The boreal forest comes to the Burke
OPENED SAT NOV 7, RUNS TO APR 25  ·  TUE–SUN 10AM–5PM  ·  ADULTS $24  ·  BURKE MUSEUM, UNIVERSITY OF WASHINGTON CAMPUS
Knowing Nature: Stories of the Boreal Forest opened yesterday in the second-floor special gallery: the northern forest belt that circles the globe above us — caribou, loons, wolves, glacial moraines — told through photography, interactive displays, first-person stories, Burke collection pieces and new work by Indigenous artists (beaded earrings, handmade snowshoes). It runs through April, so no rush, but it is a natural fit for a damp afternoon: today clears by about 11am, and the museum is closed Mondays. Free on the first Thursday of each month (next: Dec 3, open till 8pm) if you’d rather wait.

MONDAY OR TUESDAY · NEW · FILM
Agnès Varda’s Vagabond on a big screen
MON NOV 9 & TUE NOV 10, 7PM  ·  $15 ($7 MEMBERS)  ·  NORTHWEST FILM FORUM, CAPITOL HILL
Varda’s 1985 drama opens with a young drifter, Mona, found frozen in a ditch in the French countryside, then pieces together her last winter through the people who crossed her path; Sandrine Bonnaire won France’s best-actress award for it. A feature-length repertory screening at 7, so home around 9. Both nights are rainy (93–96%) — good cinema weather. Monday alternative for the urbanist in you: the architecture Honor Awards, the state’s best new buildings juried on stage, Town Hall, 7pm, $10–60.
