NATURE · MON–THU NIGHTS · NEAR MIDNIGHT · EXTREME LOW TIDES
The deepest tides of the fall: −2.2 to −3.0 feet
MON OCT 26 11:50PM −2.2 FT  ·  WED OCT 28 12:31AM −2.8 FT  ·  THU OCT 29 1:16AM −3.0 FT  ·  NOAA SEATTLE STATION
The lowest water since summer, and among the lowest of the year — but in fall the big minus tides fall at night, so these are headlamp-and-boots outings, well past bedtime. If one is worth it, take the Thursday 1:16am −3.0 ft low: the water is already well below zero by midnight, so a walk from about 11:30pm to 1am at Constellation Park on Alki or the Lincoln Park beach shows sea stars, moon snails and anemones normally out of reach. The weekend lead-in is gentler (−1.1 ft at 11:13pm Sunday). Watch your footing, go with a friend, and step around the animals.
