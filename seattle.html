SUNDAY · NEW · WORKING WATERFRONT
A Ballard Story: Pacific Fishermen, the plan for the soaker
SUN NOV 15, 10AM–5PM (RUNS TO FEB 28)  ·  $20 ADVANCE / $24 SAME DAY  ·  NATIONAL NORDIC MUSEUM, BALLARD
Sunday is 98% rain with a south wind of 8–14 mph, so this is the indoor day. New to the brief: an exhibition on Pacific Fishermen, one of Ballard’s last working shipyards on Salmon Bay, told through historic photographs, oral histories and artifacts — shipbuilding and commercial fishing on the bay, the multigenerational fishing families, and the dangers of the work. You paddle and fish past that yard; this is the inside of it. Included with general admission. The museum is open Tuesday–Sunday 10–5 (Thursdays to 8, though it closes early at 5 this coming Thursday), and the show runs through February, so there is no rush beyond the weather.
