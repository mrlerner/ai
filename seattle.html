Thomas Frank on “The Creativity Con”
WED OCT 28  ·  DOORS 6:30, TALK 7:30, OUT ~8:45  ·  $5 + OPTIONAL BOOK  ·  TOWN HALL SEATTLE, FIRST HILL
The author of What’s the Matter with Kansas? argues that “creativity” and “innovation” became the polite words executives, technologists and, in his telling, urban planners use for policies that widen the class divide. A pointed counterweight to the usual civic-tech optimism, and worth hearing as someone who builds that software. Wednesday evening is 89% rain; $5 buys a warm room and you’re out by about 8:45. The Rise and Fall of the Kingdome
MON–FRI THROUGH OCT 30  ·  8:30AM–4PM  ·  FREE  ·  KING STREET CENTER, 201 S JACKSON ST, PIONEER SQUARE
Still up through Friday in the lobby of the county office building beside the old stadium site: King County Archives photographs of the dome rising in the 1970s, the games and concerts, and the March 2000 implosion. A short rainy-weekday stop for anyone who likes public records and how a city builds and unbuilds its big projects. Office hours only.
