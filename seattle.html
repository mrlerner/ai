FRI NOV 27 · NEW · IN YOUR LANE + A BRANCH-OUT

FRI NOV 27  ·  NEPTUNE DOORS 7PM, PRICE NOT ON VENUE PAGE  ·  TOWN HALL 7:30PM, $45–119
Two guitar shows the night after Thanksgiving, both found this morning. Haley Heynderickx, the Portland songwriter, plays the Neptune Theatre in the University District; the venue calls it a rare run of solo performances after a year touring her collaboration with Max García Conover. Quiet, fingerpicked, close-miked songs that sit next to Big Thief and Aldous Harding on your shelf (my comparison). Doors 7pm; the page prints no show time, end time or price, so expect it to run past 9. The branch-out: Yamandu Costa, Town Hall Great Hall, 7:30pm, $45–119. Town Hall quotes the conductor Kurt Masur calling him “the Paganini of the guitar”; he plays Brazilian seven-string, fast and ornate, nearer to Rodrigo y Gabriela than to a folk night. A concert, so the 75-minute talk rule does not apply.
