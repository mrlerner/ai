THURSDAY · NEW · FILM
The Parallax View, the paranoid thriller that opens on the Space Needle
THU NOV 12, 7PM  ·  $15 ($10 SENIOR/STUDENT, $7 MEMBERS)  ·  NORTHWEST FILM FORUM, CAPITOL HILL
Alan J. Pakula’s 1974 conspiracy film: after a presidential candidate is shot atop the Space Needle, Warren Beatty’s reporter starts to suspect the shadowy Parallax Corporation. A Seattle-set landmark of 70s cinema, one show only, 7pm so you are out around 9. Thursday is the iffy day (52%), so a natural indoor plan. Same night in your lane: Triptides, the Los Angeles jangle-psych band that sits next to the Allah-Las, Substation in Ballard, 7pm, $25.87 all-in.
FRIDAY · NEW · BRANCH-OUT MUSIC
Flamenco guitar, song and dance, one night only
FRI NOV 13, DOORS 6:30, SHOW 7:30  ·  $30 ADVANCE / $35 DOOR  ·  THE ROYAL ROOM, COLUMBIA CITY
Seattle troupe Oleaje Flamenco’s Aire y Fuego is a one-night production built around two singers, Daniel Azcarate and Stephanie Pedraza, with Jed Miley on guitar, dancers Amelia Moore and Monica Mota, and percussionist Kyle McCurdy. The branch-out: if you like the Spanish-guitar side of Hermanos Gutiérrez, this is the raw, live source — fierce fingerwork under handclaps and footwork. Advance tickets online only; seating first come, so arrive near doors. (Son Volt, the alt-country band that sits right by Neil Young, plays the Crocodile the same night — sold out, waitlist only.)
