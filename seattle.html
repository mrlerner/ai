MON OCT 5, DOORS 6:30, SHOW 7:30  ·  $20 ADVANCE / $25 DOOR  ·  THE ROYAL ROOM, 5000 RAINIER AVE S, COLUMBIA CITY  ·  ALL AGES UNTIL 10
New on the Royal Room calendar, and it is the week’s in-your-lanes music pick: Del Rey, who the venue’s page (quoting Acoustic Guitar) calls “one of the best fingerpickers of this or any generation,” playing a syncopated style built on prewar blues and barrelhouse piano. If Steve Gunn’s open tunings and the Nick Drake fingerpickers are the thing, this is the pre-history of it, played on resonator and ukulele by someone who has spent decades at it.
