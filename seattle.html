The first dark afternoon of the year: go see the chum in Piper’s Creek before 3
SAT & SUN 11AM–3PM, WED 2–4PM, THROUGH DEC 6  ·  FREE  ·  CARKEEK PARK, NORTHWEST SEATTLE
Clocks fell back overnight, so tonight it’s dark by about 5. The midday is the best light of the week: the hourly forecast drops from 64% cloud at 11am to 29% at 1pm and 24% at 3pm, no rain, high 53. Volunteer Salmon Stewards are on the creek bank 11–3 pointing out spawning chum and explaining the run. Aim for 1pm. The rest of the weekend window closes Wednesday, when rain moves in from late afternoon; the Wednesday 2–4 steward shift should still be dry (12%).
Indoor alternative, same hours: Battling Bloodsuckers, a National Geographic explorer on why bats and mosquitoes drink blood, Benaroya Hall, downtown, 2pm, from $33 plus fees (also Mon and Tue 7:30).

WED · IDEAS · 7:30PM · $10–124
Claudia Rankine on Triage, her first book after Citizen
WED NOV 4  ·  7:30PM, OUT ABOUT 8:45  ·  $10–124  ·  TOWN HALL GREAT HALL, FIRST HILL
Rankine is the poet whose Citizen became a defining book on race in America. The new one is a personal narrative about a long, rocky friendship between two women who called themselves sisters. A rainy evening (73%), so indoors is the right call.
Same night, later, in your lane: Big Star Quintet: the 1970s Memphis power-pop band’s songs, which Teenage Fanclub built a sound on, played by Mike Mills of R.E.M., Chris Stamey and Jon Auer; Neptune Theatre, University District, doors 7, show 8.
THU · IN YOUR LANE · NEW · 8PM · $55.30 ALL-IN
The Tallest Man on Earth at the Crocodile
THU NOV 5  ·  DOORS 7, SHOW 8  ·  $40 + $15.30 FEES, 21+, GENERAL ADMISSION  ·  THE CROCODILE, BELLTOWN
Kristian Matsson, a Swedish singer with a fingerpicked guitar and a voice that keeps drawing early-Dylan comparisons. He’s touring Just Beyond Endless Mountain, out Oct 9, recorded after moving back to the Swedish countryside where he grew up. Sits squarely between your Dylan and Nick Drake–style fingerpickers. Andrea von Kampen, a Nebraska folk singer, is also on the bill. Ticketweb showed tickets available; 8pm is late for you, and no end time is posted.
Earlier, free, same night: Valeria Luiselli, the novelist of Lost Children Archive, on Beginning Middle End, a mother and daughter’s trip through Sicily; Town Hall, 7:30. Fiddler’s Inn (Wedgwood tavern) has live music Thursday as always, but its November lineup isn’t posted yet; October shows started at 7.

FRI · BRANCH-OUT OF THE WEEK · 7:30PM · $25–50 SLIDING
Or the gentler Friday: Corey Harris and Cedric Watson, blues guitar meets Creole fiddle
FRI NOV 6  ·  7:30PM, A FULL CONCERT  ·  $25–50 SLIDING SCALE  ·  TOWN HALL GREAT HALL, FIRST HILL
