PLANNED · SEPT 11–13

2 NIGHTS · GUEMES → CYPRESS · PADDLE + COHO

Cypress Island

Launches from the cabin’s own beach: two slack-timed crossings of Bellingham Channel that double as troll lines through the mid-September coho peak. Camps at Pelican Beach and Cypress Head, hour-by-hour currents, full itinerary.

SEPT 12–19 · 2 NIGHTS · SAN JUANS

Matia & Sucia

Mid-September weekdays flip these islands: the powerboats thin out and every coho hooked is a keeper. North Beach to Rolfe Cove’s old-growth loop, then Sucia’s fingers and the tide seams off Ewing Cove.

ANY CALM EVENING · FISH PEAK SEPT 8–20

Blake Island

A state park you can only reach by water, twenty minutes from the city. Same systems, same fishing, one exit off the highway — any calm evening works, dusk troll off the south-end rips included.

SEPT 1–20 · SUNSHINE COAST, BC

Copeland Islands

Arbutus-topped islets at the doorstep of Desolation Sound with none of its commitment. Two ferries and most of a day north; then short hops from Lund, warm(ish) swims, and peak coho on the evening troll.

SEPT 5–20 · ADVANCED · DISCOVERY ISLANDS, BC

Octopus Islands

A wilderness marine park locked behind tidal rapids, reachable only when the ocean says so. File it for after the self-rescue class and a few Cypress loops — then thread Beazley Pass at slack.
