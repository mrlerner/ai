Sunday 9/13 — Fish, then cross — two slack windows

CROSS AT 8:10 AM — OR 2:45 PM

Cabin cleaning can happen any time Sunday, so there are two ways home. Plan A: pack first, fish the rips, troll the seam across on the 8:10 slack — done by 10 with the whole day left. Plan B: fish the morning, laze, cross on the 2:45 slack (window ~2:00–3:30). Between windows the channel is shut — ebb peaks 3.3 kn at 11:15.

No in-between

Fishing & fine print

Regs (Marine Area 7, September): any coho may be kept Sept 1–30, limit 2 salmon, release Chinook, sockeye, and chum. Barbless single-point hooks. WA licence + catch record card. Bleed fish immediately.

Water: no water source on Cypress (DNR) — pack it all: 3–4 L per person per day, ~8 L each for the weekend, plus a group reserve. Split it across boats; bags laid low in the hull double as ballast.

Fishing, per the research: the documented coho edge is low light (early morning, overcast) and tide rips where currents converge (Riptidefish; WDFW MA 7). No published evidence for or against an evening bite here — dusk casts are experiments. Fishing blocks are slack-timed for kayak safety, not because slack is a proven bite trigger.

Weather: check the Rosario Strait marine forecast Saturday night — wind against Sunday’s ebb moves the go/no-go call more than the tide table does.
