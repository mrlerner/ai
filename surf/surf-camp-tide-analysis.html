Recommendation

Top pick: Mar 13–20 — 5 out of 7 days have morning high tides between 6:00–10:00 AM. The sweet spot lands right in the middle of your week, so you'll have ideal morning surf conditions almost every day.

Runner-up: Feb 13–20 — 4 great morning tide days front-loaded at the start of the week. Also a strong choice.

Avoid: Mar 6–13 and Mar 20–27 — These weeks land right on a new/full moon, meaning morning high tides are at 2–5 AM (too early and dark). You'd be surfing low tide or rising tide all morning.
