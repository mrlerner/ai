FANTASY TIER · SNORKEL + FREEDIVE + LEARNER SURF · THE CORAL TRIANGLE AND BEYOND

The Coral File

The brief, sharpened: see great coral while great coral still exists — from the surface, and on one breath — with a wave a learner can ride somewhere in the same trip. The South Pacific File graded six islands on culture, reef and surf in equal parts. This file regrades the map for the reef alone, and pulls the Coral Triangle onto it: Indonesia, the Philippines, Papua New Guinea, the Solomons and Timor-Leste, where three-quarters of the world’s coral species live.

Every entry answers three questions: how good is the reef from the surface, does it work on one breath, and where is the learner wave.

THE SNORKELER, HONESTLY

A strong snorkeler, not yet a freediver. The plan that unlocks this whole page is a two-day freediving course on the front end of the trip (AIDA 2 or the SSI equivalent: relaxation, equalization, safe buddy rules, 10–15 m). After that, the best reef is the reef between 3 and 15 m in clear, calm water with good light — not the deep wall. Surf profile unchanged from the South Pacific File: comfortable to about 5-foot faces, still building turns, wants forgiving waves.

Before it’s too late · what the bleaching actually did

THE FOURTH GLOBAL BLEACHING EVENT · 2023–2025

The largest bleaching event on record ran from early 2023 to mid-2025. NOAA counts bleaching-level heat stress on about 84% of the world’s reef area, in all three ocean basins, and says the event likely ended in 2025. The South Pacific was hit first and hard: Fiji, Vanuatu, Samoa and French Polynesia all saw mass bleaching in 2023–24, and Fiji had its first back-to-back years. In French Polynesia an 80-day marine heatwave in early 2024 left a surveyed Tuamotu atoll with 27–43% of colonies recently dead by May 2024.

The counter-example is the reason this page exists. At Yenbuba in Raja Ampat, more than 70% of the dominant coral bleached at the December 2024 peak; by November 2025 it was under 20%, with widespread repigmentation. Researchers credit strong currents, deep-water upwelling, healthy grazing-fish populations, intact mangroves, and shallow inlets that act as heat-training grounds for coral. Kimbe Bay, Alor, Komodo and Ataúro share most of those traits. The lesson for a trip: pick reefs with structural resilience — current, depth, protection, fish — not a single pretty lagoon, and go in the next two or three seasons, not the next ten.

The six

1

Raja Ampat

KRI + ARBOREK HOMESTAYS, WEST PAPUA · THE REEF, FULL STOP

The epicenter of the Coral Triangle: the Bird’s Head Seascape holds 574 coral species, roughly three-quarters of every species on Earth. You sleep in a village homestay on stilts and the house reef starts at the end of the jetty. There is no learner wave within a day of here — Lombok on the way home is the surf.

Getting there
SEA to Jakarta or Bali via Taipei/Tokyo; onward to Sorong (Jakarta nonstop ~4 h, or via Makassar); the public ferry to Waisai runs twice daily and takes about 2 h; the homestay boat meets you. Budget two travel days each way.
Under the mask
Jetty reefs on Kri and Arborek with 10–30 m off the end; Cape Kri’s fish-count record; Manta Sandy; Yenbuba’s coral slope, the one that bleached and came back. Snorkel and freedive trips run from the homestays without a tank.
On one breath
Dedicated freedive safaris base on Kri and Arborek (Freedive Nusa runs them from Bali; a local outfit, Raja Ampat Freediving, runs land-based weeks). Calm mornings, warm water, coral from the surface down.
Your surf
None here. The pairing is Lombok’s Selong Belanak on the way home (see entry 3), or a Bali beach-break week at Canggu/Batu Bolong if the flights route that way.
When
October–April: calm seas, best visibility, 27–30°C water. The mid-year windy season closes some islands.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

Reef and freediving score as high as anything on Earth. Surf scores zero, honestly, and the fix is a two-base trip: freedive course in Bali or Nusa Penida, a homestay week on Kri, then a Lombok learner week before the flight home. Long, but every leg is doing a job.

Pros

  • The highest coral diversity on the planet, and the best-documented recovery from the 2024 bleaching
  • House reefs off the jetty: the best snorkeling requires no boat, no schedule
  • Homestay money funds village reef patrols — your stay pays the right people
  • Freedive-specific trips already exist; you are not inventing the format

Cons

  • Zero surf; the wave is an extra week and another flight away
  • Two travel days each way, and Sorong connections reshuffle
  • Homestays are honest: mattress on a floor, bucket showers, generator hours
  • Park fee, boat fuel and transfers add up faster than the room rate suggests

VS THE BRIEF · the reef you are trying to see before it’s too late is this one — and it is the one that came back.

2

Papatura, Solomon Islands

SANTA ISABEL (+ MAROVO LAGOON) · BOTH, FROM ONE DOCK

A small island resort off Santa Isabel where the same boat runs a beginner reef wave in the morning and hard-and-soft coral gardens after lunch. The Solomons are the Coral Triangle’s forgotten corner: the same swells that hit Hawai‘i arrive a few days later, and almost nobody is here.

Getting there
SEA to Brisbane or Nadi, then Honiara (Brisbane ~3 h, Nadi ~2 h). Solomon Airlines flies Honiara–Suavanao five days a week; the resort boat is 10 minutes. Two travel days each way.
Your surf
Tarzan’s, a right-and-left peak the resort calls its family wave, and Dolphins, a small left. Nine breaks total, most within 20 minutes by boat, typically 3–6 ft. Guided surf runs about AUD 100 a day for two sessions.
Under the mask
Hard and soft coral gardens off the resort, snorkel gear on hand, kayaks and SUPs free. Marovo Lagoon, one flight away, has what guides call the finest coral gardens in the South Pacific plus WWII wrecks in the shallows.
On one breath
No freedive school — bring the certification. Calm lagoon water and reef tops at 5–15 m make it easy solo-buddy territory.
When
The two seasons fight: surf runs late November–April on North Pacific swell; the calm, dry snorkel season is April–November. The overlap is late November or April, and cyclone season is November–April.
Health
High-risk malaria zone, year-round: prophylaxis is not optional. US advisory sits at Level 1.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

The only entry where reef and learner wave share a dock. The reef is a notch below Raja Ampat and the wave a notch below Lombok, but the combination is the brief written as a place. Aim the week at the April shoulder: swell still running, seas calming, malaria pills in the bag.

Pros

  • Beginner reef wave and world-class snorkel in the same boat day
  • Emptiest lineups in this file — a capped resort, no town, no crowd
  • Hawai‘i’s swell three days late, in 29°C water
  • Marovo Lagoon extension turns a good week into a great two

Cons

  • Surf season and calm-sea season barely overlap; you pick a shoulder and hope
  • Malaria prophylaxis and real remoteness
  • Resort-only economy: fewer culture days than the South Pacific File offers
  • Solomon Airlines schedules move; build slack into both ends

VS THE BRIEF · the both-at-once answer — less reef than Raja Ampat, less wave than Lombok, and the only place that delivers both before breakfast.

3

Komodo + Lombok

LABUAN BAJO + SELONG BELANAK, INDONESIA · THE CLEAN PAIRING

Two bases, one 90-minute flight apart, each the best-in-class version of its half. Selong Belanak is the finest sand-bottom learner wave in Indonesia. Komodo’s current-fed reefs put mantas, coral gardens and a pink beach inside snorkeling range. Freedive schools on Nusa Penida and the Gilis sit on the route between them.

Getting there
SEA to Bali via Taipei/Tokyo/Singapore (~20 h). Lombok is a 30-minute hop or a fast boat; Labuan Bajo is 1 h 15 from Bali or Lombok. The gentlest logistics in the Triangle.
Your surf
Selong Belanak’s sand-bottom rollers with schools on the beach; Don Don’s and Inside Gerupuk (soft A-frames in a sheltered bay); Tanjung Aan. October–April is the learner season — smaller, cleaner mornings, cheaper rooms. May–September is bigger and better for the next version of you.
Under the mask
Komodo from Labuan Bajo: Manta Point, Sebayur’s healthy coral, Siaba Besar’s turtles, Pink Beach gardens. A 3-day/2-night boat covers it. Nusa Tenggara bleached in 2024 — expect patchiness on the shallowest flats and thriving slopes where the current runs.
On one breath
Freedive Nusa on Nusa Penida (Manta Point there too, and Crystal Bay’s hard-coral wall, the healthiest left in southern Bali), or the Gili islands’ cheap, well-regarded courses. Do the course first; the Komodo boat is the exam.
When
Komodo is best April–November; Lombok learner surf is best October–April. The overlaps: October–November, or April. Both are excellent months in both places.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

Passes all three with the least friction of anything in this file. The trade is that Komodo’s reef, extraordinary as it is, sits a tier below Raja Ampat, and Komodo’s currents mean the best sites are guided drift snorkels, not lazy floats. Ten days, three bases, one country, no malaria pills.

Pros

  • The best learner wave in Indonesia and a manta channel in one itinerary
  • Short domestic hops; a freedive school on the route between them
  • Two shoulder windows where both halves are in season
  • Cheap once landed; surf camps and liveaboards at every budget

Cons

  • Komodo’s shallow flats took 2024 bleaching; the slopes are the show
  • Currents make the best sites guided, not free-range
  • Labuan Bajo is busy and getting busier; book the boat, not the day tour
  • Bali transit adds a day each way

VS THE BRIEF · the itinerary you could book tomorrow — the reef a notch down from the record, the wave exactly right.

4

Siargao + Southern Leyte

GENERAL LUNA + SOGOD BAY, PHILIPPINES · THE SURF-FIRST VERSION

Siargao is the Philippines’ surf capital, and the beginner wave sits a hundred meters from the famous one. Across the strait, Sogod Bay has walls divers rank among Asia’s best and wild whale sharks you may only meet by snorkeling. Cebu’s Moalboal — sardine ball, freedive schools — is the third leg if the trip stretches.

Getting there
SEA to Manila or Cebu (via Tokyo/Taipei, ~17–20 h), then a domestic flight to Siargao. Siargao to Southern Leyte is a fast craft to Surigao and a 3-hour ferry to Liloan — a full day — or a short flight via Cebu.
Your surf
Jacking Horse, a soft beginner-to-intermediate peak beside Cloud 9; Guyam and Rock Island on small days. Surf season September–March; peak power in September–October, friendliest for learners November–March. Cloud 9 itself: boardwalk, camera, no leash.
Under the mask
Sogod Bay: Napantao’s wall, 20–30 m visibility, and wild whale sharks off Pintuyan November–April, snorkel only since 2009. Apo Island’s community reserve (650 fish species) and Moalboal’s sardines are a Cebu hop away.
On one breath
Moalboal is a freediving town: Mermaid Point for the course, Pescador Island for the graduation. Southern Leyte has its own freedive-specific operators for the walls and the sharks.
When
November–March: learner surf on Siargao, whale sharks and calm seas in Sogod Bay, the whole Visayas in dry season. Typhoon exposure is real July–December; Siargao was flattened by Odette in December 2021.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

Surf leads and reef follows, which is the inverse of Raja Ampat. The coral is very good rather than record-setting; the freediving culture is the best in this file; the whale sharks are the memory. The catch is a long transit day between the two halves.

Pros

  • Daily learner waves in a real surf town, beside the famous one
  • Wild whale sharks and walls a ferry away; sardines and freedive schools a hop away
  • English everywhere, cheap once landed, strong dive infrastructure
  • The November–March window is exactly when the PNW is worst

Cons

  • Reef a clear tier below Raja Ampat or Kimbe
  • Transit day between Siargao and Leyte, or a backtrack through Cebu
  • Typhoon season shadows the surf season’s front half
  • Siargao has been discovered; General Luna is busy in season

VS THE BRIEF · if the board comes first and the mask second, this is the file’s best answer — a real surf town with a whale shark bay across the water.

5

Ataúro, Timor-Leste

BELOI + ADARA · THE RECORD HOLDER

A 2016 Conservation International survey counted an average of 253 reef-fish species per site around Ataúro — the highest ever recorded anywhere, beating Raja Ampat’s own record. Deep cold water in the Wetar Strait feeds the reef; walls drop off meters from the beach. A handful of eco-lodges, a village of women freedivers, and almost no tourists.

Getting there
Fly to Dili from Bali or Darwin. Then the Nakroma ferry (Saturdays, ~3 h, a few dollars), the Laju Laju (Thursdays), a weekend fast boat (1 h), or a 15-minute MAF flight on Mon/Wed/Fri. The schedule sets your week for you.
Under the mask
Coral Garden for the shallow snorkel; Adara Wall dropping off meters from the beach; Watuai, the house reef one dive magazine ranked in the world’s top ten. Eleven named sites, most a short boat ride or a swim from shore.
On one breath
The Wawata Topu, the women spearfishing freedivers of Adara village, are the island’s tradition and its 2016 documentary. Deep water close in; bring the certification, there is no school.
Your surf
Honest answer: a footnote. Small reef setups east and west of Dili, inconsistent, beginner season October–May — and saltwater crocodiles outside the capital are a real hazard. Surf is a Bali stopover, not an Ataúro plan.
When
Dry season May–November for calm crossings; visibility is strong year-round thanks to the deep strait.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

Reef and freediving at the very top of the file, with the added pull of being the place nobody has heard of. Surf fails outright. Treat it as Raja Ampat’s quieter, cheaper, closer-to-Bali cousin — and pair it with the same Lombok week.

Pros

  • The highest reef-fish diversity ever surveyed, and you can reach it from the beach
  • Deep-strait upwelling: cool water, big visibility, structural bleaching resistance
  • Eco-lodges at village prices; one Bali flight closer than Raja Ampat
  • A living freediving culture to learn from

Cons

  • No learner surf worth planning around; crocodile risk on the mainland coast
  • Ferry days dictate the itinerary
  • Thin infrastructure; bring everything, expect generator hours
  • Fewer boat operators means fewer weather backups

VS THE BRIEF · the record, quietly held — go for the reef, accept that the board stays in Bali.

6

Kimbe Bay, PNG

WALINDI, WEST NEW BRITAIN · THE SNORKELER’S DIVE CAPITAL

The reefs here rise to within a meter of the surface, which is why guides say Kimbe is one of the few places where the snorkeling matches the diving. About 900 fish species and 400 corals in one bay, a dozen visiting whale and dolphin species, and a single plantation resort that has run it for forty years.

Getting there
SEA to Brisbane, to Port Moresby, to Hoskins, then an hour by road. Two travel days each way — the same pilgrimage as Kavieng in the South Pacific File.
Under the mask
Reef tops at 1–5 m over walls, so the snorkel sees what the tank sees. Walindi runs dedicated snorkel boats; the bay is calm most of the year.
On one breath
Nearly ideal terrain: coral-topped pinnacles with clean drop-offs, warm still water, and boats that stay with you. No school; bring the cert.
Your surf
None nearby. PNG’s learner surf story is the one the South Pacific File already told about Kavieng: not yet.
When
Year-round; the driest, calmest months are roughly May–November. Malaria prophylaxis required.
The balance test · reef + one breath + learner surf

Reef and freediving at the top of the file; surf absent; travel enormous. Kimbe is the answer if the question narrows to “where is the best coral I can see from the surface,” and the wrong answer if the board is coming.

Pros

  • Snorkeling as good as the diving, by design of the reef itself
  • More reef species than the whole Caribbean, in one bay
  • One resort, one boat, forty years of knowing every bommie
  • Pairs with Kavieng someday, when the turns arrive

Cons

  • Hardest travel in the file after Raja Ampat, and no surf at the end of it
  • Malaria zone; remote medical backup
  • Single-resort pricing
  • Evenings are the resort, full stop

VS THE BRIEF · the purest reef trip on the page — file it beside Kavieng for the year the two PNG trips become one.

Wildcards

Alor, Indonesia

Cold upwellings, spectacular walls and some of the healthiest hard coral in the Triangle, with a small freedive-and-bungalow operation on Kepa island. No surf; a domestic hop from Bali via Kupang. Freedive Nusa runs expeditions here. The dark-horse reef pick.

Wakatobi, Sulawesi

A single luxury resort with unlimited house-reef access, three snorkel boats a day, and a private charter flight from Bali. Roughly 950 fish and 450 coral species in the park. The most comfortable coral on this page, at the highest price. No surf.

Bunaken, North Sulawesi

Protected since 1991; walls with turtles and clouds of fish a short boat from Manado. Coral inside the park is healthier than out, but city plastic and early bleaching are showing. Cheap, accessible, a notch below Raja Ampat.

Bali: Amed + Nusa Penida

Not the reef destination — the classroom. Amed for the freediving course over the Liberty wreck, Nusa Penida for mantas and Crystal Bay’s hard-coral wall, Canggu and Batu Bolong for beginner beach breaks. Every Indonesian option above routes through here anyway.

What this does to the South Pacific File

RE-SCORED FOR REEF ONLY · Tahiti + the Tuamotus keeps the best pass snorkel on Earth, but French Polynesia’s 2024 heatwave killed a quarter or more of shallow colonies at surveyed atolls, and Moorea’s recovery has stalled. Check current reports before booking around Fakarava. Fiji bleached two years running (2023–24, into 2025 at some sites); the soft-coral capital may still be the soft-coral capital, but verify Rainbow Reef and Namena conditions with the dive shops that season. Vanuatu was among the first to bleach in 2023; its aces (the Coolidge, Million Dollar Point, the blue holes) are wrecks and freshwater, not coral, so the grade holds. Samoa and Tonga were never coral-first. Kavieng is PNG, and PNG is where the coral answer lives — Kimbe Bay is its snorkel-first sibling.

Net: for culture, the South Pacific File stands. For coral, the map shifts two thousand miles west.

The call

IF THE REEF IS THE POINT · Raja Ampat, October–April. Freedive course on Nusa Penida first, a homestay week on Kri, then Selong Belanak on the way home. Three weeks, three jobs, and the reef that came back.

IF BOTH IN ONE PLACE · Papatura, in April. The only entry where the learner wave and the coral garden share a dock. Malaria pills, cyclone-season awareness, and nobody else in the lineup.

IF SIMPLEST LOGISTICS WIN · Komodo + Lombok. The trip you could book tomorrow: the best learner wave in Indonesia, a manta channel, a freedive school between them, no pills, two shoulder windows.

IF SURF LEADS · Siargao + Sogod Bay, November–March. A real surf town, a beginner peak beside Cloud 9, and wild whale sharks a ferry away — in the months the PNW is coldest.

IF THE RECORD MATTERS · Ataúro. The most fish ever counted on a reef, reachable from the beach, in a place nobody you know has been.

FILE FOR LATER · Kimbe Bay. The purest coral trip on the page, filed beside Kavieng for the year PNG gets two weeks.
